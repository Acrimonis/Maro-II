# Regulated Zones — Practical ELI16 Summary

## Your Boat: < 6 m

72 regulation zones exist in the Nice-Fréjus corridor. **69 apply to you** — only the 3 "mandatory pilot service" zones (for ships > 50 m) don't.

## 🚤 Speed Limits — What Matters Most

| Where | Limit | Detail |
|-------|-------|--------|
| **Within 300 m of ANY coast** | **5 knots** | This is the French "bande des 300 m" — already tracked by the Zone300 feature. Whole coastline. |
| **Golfe Juan / Cap d'Antibes** (43.58°N, 7.04°E) | **10 knots** | The bay between Cap d'Antibes and the Lérins islands. |
| **Off Nice** (43.58°N, 7.13°E) | **5 knots** | Near the port / Baie des Anges. |
| **Off Nice** (43.58°N, 7.14°E) | **10 knots** | Slightly further out. |
| **Outside marked channels** | **3 knots** | Applies in confined areas (harbour approaches). |
| Various fairway zones | 5–10 kn | Channel approaches with navigation markers. |

**Bottom line:** If you're within 300 m of shore, you're at 5 kn everywhere. Outside that, Cap d'Antibes and Baie des Anges keep you at 10 kn.

## ⚓ Anchoring

**Applies to you (small boat):**
- Area around 43.545°N, 7.054°E (between Ste-Marguerite and mainland): anchoring prohibited
- Some zones say "anchoring prohibited for vessels > 20 m" — those do NOT apply to you (you're < 6 m, you're fine)

**Exempt (not your problem):**
- Anchoring restrictions for vessels > 20 m / > 24 m — you're exempt
- Mandatory anchoring areas for 24-80 m ships — you're exempt
- Mandatory anchoring for > 80 m ships — you're exempt
