# Route — the marker cost, into the acquisition: a priced marker and a wall

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Status:** implemented — ordered by the user's word, *"plan the integration of markers with route cost to route acquisition"*, and landed on `feature/rte-mark-cost` (see `## Implemented`).

## Why

- **The marker side shipped its half and the engine never read it.** [`UserMarker.routingCost`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/data/model/markers/UserMarker.kt:63) is a price 1–9 or the wall 10 (`ROUTING_COST_BLOCKED`), guarded by [`validRoutingCost`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/data/model/markers/UserMarker.kt:160), set by the wizard's slider — and its KDoc still says *"a routing engine that does not exist yet"*. Nothing in `spatial/` imports a marker.
- **The cost field already names the seat.** The [`RouteCostField`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteCostField.kt:260) KDoc lists *"a marker weight"* among the soft sources and states the invariant a marker must obey: a source only **adds** to the base, never replaces it, so *"a marker makes the sea dearer or leaves it alone"*.
- **The parked item is this item.** The feature's own todo — *"Phase 5's marker weights … the weight scaling the per-metre price inside a circle's radius or a corridor's band and never replacing the base, merged with the shipped marker scale's wall into one scale"* — is exactly what the shipped `routingCost` has been waiting for. This plan realises it; on landing it closes that todo.
- **The acquisition prices water in two reads that must agree.** The A* reads `sourceCostSec` the rasterizer wrote per cell; the pull's guard prices the field's soft sources per point. The code's own invariant is *"the search and the guard can never disagree"*, so a marker must reach both through the one law.

## The shape of the integration

- **A priced marker (1–9) is one soft source.** Inside its geometry the sea costs more; outside it costs nothing. The law is **linear** — the base per-metre cost scaled by the marker's step: `markerPriceSec(cellM, paceKn, step) = baseCostSec(cellM, paceKn) × step × strength`, the `strength` the Routing-settings row's value **threaded as a live provider beside `pace`** — never a static `AppConfig` read — so a slider move reaches the next arm. It **adds** to the base and never replaces it, so no passable cell drops below base and the A*'s closed set stays valid — the field's own invariant.
- **A wall marker (10) is one hard source**, rastered the way the depth gate is ([`depthGateSource`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteCostField.kt:229)): `blockedAt` = inside the geometry, `distanceAt` = `0.0` where blocked, `MAX_VALUE` otherwise. The rasterizer already paints rastered walls generically, and the pull refuses a chord through one. The wall stands **whatever the price switch says** — `route.marker.enabled` prices water, it never lifts a wall. A wall the line must cross is a **`NO_PATH`, never a reported forced crossing** — the forced-crossing probe blocks zones alone, and this wall is a hard cell the search cannot enter.
- **The price is λ-free, deliberately.** A marker is the user's own rule, not a Driving preference, so all three rungs price it alike — unlike the band and the zones, whose limits ride the grid and are priced per expansion at the pass's λ. That is what lets the marker price be baked into `sourceCostSec` at rasterisation once per arm, instead of needing a limit channel the grid does not have for it.
- **The geometry the law reads:**
  - **Circle** — inside `center` at `radiusM`; its price boundary is the circle itself, so the declared clearance is `|distToCenter − radiusM|`.
  - **Corridor** — within `widthM / 2` of the `p1–p2` segment, rounded caps included; the clearance is `|distToSegment − widthM / 2|`, and the **cap circles** are part of that distance or the declaration lies.
  - **Pin** — out of the cost: a Pin carries no price and no wall, so the engine never reads one and the wizard hides the cost from it.
- **Both fields carry the same markers.** The **grid field** ([`RouteGridBuilder`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridBuilder.kt:112) and `:221`) gains the marker sources so the rasterizer writes the wall as `LAND` and bakes the price into `sourceCostSec`; the **guard field** ([`RoutePassRunner`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePassRunner.kt:78) and [`RouteFinePass`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteFinePass.kt:86)) gains the same sources so the pull prices and refuses identically.

## Plumbing — how a marker reaches the engine

- **The projection, not the model.** [`MultipassWorld`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassWorld.kt:23) gains one marker member in the feature's own vocabulary — a `RouteMarker` shape in `spatial/multipass` carrying the geometry, the cost step and the wall flag — so `spatial/` imports no `UserMarker`, the same decoupling `RouteEndSelection` already states. The projection drops **Pins and `IDLE_AUTO` at the world**, never at the field: only a Circle or Corridor that carries a cost becomes a `RouteMarker`.
- **The live adapter gains a provider**, exactly as `zonesProvider` does today: [`LiveMultipassWorld`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassWorld.kt:144) takes `markersProvider: () -> List<UserMarker>` and projects it to `RouteMarker`s on read.
- **The wiring is one line in the composition root** — [`MapScreen.kt:743`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/MapScreen.kt:743) builds `LiveMultipassWorld`, and `markersViewModel.allMarkers` is already collected at `:1166`, so the provider reads `{ markersViewModel.allMarkers.value }`.
- **The strength rides the engine factory.** [`RouteEngineChoice.factory`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteEngineChoice.kt:26) gains a strength provider beside `paceKn`, every row's lambda updates (the dummy ignores it), and both call sites pass it — [`MapScreen.kt:757`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/MapScreen.kt:757) and [`MapRouteEffects.kt:29`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/MapRouteEffects.kt:29) — then `RouteAvoidEngine` threads it into `costField` beside `pace`.
- **`costField` gains the list**, read from the world by its callers beside `zones`, already Pins- and `IDLE_AUTO`-free; the marker sources are added **only when the list is non-empty**, so a no-marker solve keeps today's fast path (`hasSoft` and `hasBlocking` both false). The dummy engine reads no field and stays untouched by construction.
- **The fine tile cache key is the costed markers themselves, with their cost.** The fine layer is rastered tile-by-tile and cached under [`TileKey`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/TileKey.kt:28); today that key carries `zoneRings` but nothing marker-shaped, and markers are mutable — a created, moved or re-costed wall after a tile is cached would serve a stale tile and draw the line through a wall. `TileKey` therefore gains the **projected marker set** — every Circle or Corridor that carries a routing cost, each with its geometry and its cost step / wall flag — built in [`fineTileKey`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridBuilder.kt:498); the set is the key material itself, so a marker's move, geometry edit or cost change moves the key and no separate stamp is owed.

## Settled decisions (2026-10-10)

- **Pins are out of route cost** — a Pin carries no price and no wall, the wizard hides the cost from a Pin, and the engine never reads one — on the user's word, *"remove cost from pin in wizard and exclude them from route cost"*.
- **λ-free.** A marker is a fixed rule, not a Driving preference: all three rungs price it alike — on the user's word, *"λ free"*.
- **The wall refuses outright.** A rastered hard source, the depth gate's shape, no standoff margin — on the user's word, *"refuse out right"*.
- **The strength is linear, with a UI row.** `markerPriceSec = baseCostSec × step × strength`, the factor a Routing-tab row beside the Driving preference raises, default `1.0`, threaded as a live provider beside `pace` — never a static `AppConfig` read — on the user's word, *"linear for now, set a UI prop to increase the strength"*. The row and its both-locale strings join Phase 5.
- **The enable switch is config-only.** `route.marker.enabled` lives in `maro.properties` behind `AppConfig` with no settings row — exactly as the band and zone switches — the strength row being the one UI knob.
- **The tag is the agent's: reuse `ZONE`.** No new [`MultipassCellState`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassGrid.kt:58) value; if a `MARKER` state is ever owed it is appended, never inserted.
- **Auto markers are out, and their whole route step goes.** The engine prices only `MarkerOrigin.USER`; `IDLE_AUTO` is skipped — and on the user's word the marker side drops the **full route step** (role and cost) from an auto marker's wizard, so an auto marker carries neither a role nor a cost. That Markers-side edit is recorded here and owned by the Markers feature.
- **The wall always stands.** `route.marker.enabled` gates the price alone; a wall (10) blocks whatever the switch says — on the user's word, *"10 no cross"*.
- **An end standing on a wall moves sea-side.** An end inside a wall marker is moved to the nearest passable water beyond it — the depth gate's own repair shape — and only the end cell is exempt from the wall, never the wall's whole geometry — on the user's word, *"1"*.

## Risks

- **A corridor's caps are a boundary too.** The declaration that omits the rounded cap circles is the plan's worst failure mode: a group straddling a cap would be proved where it must not be, silently moving the line — the same class the ring-hole review caught.
- **A wall the end stands on.** The repair, the end discs and the berth carve force-free water; a wall marker under an end must be handled by the settled decision (end moved sea-side, end cell exempt) or the carve opens a hole in the wall.
- **λ-free is baked into the shared grid.** If the λ-free decision is wrong, the price is wrong on all three rungs at once; the decision is taken before Phase 2, not after.
- **The price must stay an excess.** A marker weight that ever turned negative would break the closed-set A*; `markerPriceSec` is non-negative by construction and the test pins it.

- No per-marker toggle and no settings row beyond the one strength row — the marker side already labels the cost; this plan adds only that row.
- No `routeOrigin` / `routeDestination` work — the ends are already consumed through `RouteEndSelection`.
- The dummy engine stays a straight line and prices nothing.
