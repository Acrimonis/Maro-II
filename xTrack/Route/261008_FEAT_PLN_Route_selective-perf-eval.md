# Route — the selective engine's acquisition cost: assessment and evaluation plan

**Date:** 2026-10-08 · **Status:** in design — P0–P4.1 landed (the harness, the single-flight build, the cheapened depth reads, the flat cell, the anchored lattice), P4.2–P5 open, the device passes owed · **Order:** the user's word of 2026-10-08, `#focus route` beside `#new route-algo-selective-eval`, opened to evaluate why the selective acquisition costs more than the adaptive one · **Branch:** `feature/route-algo-selective-eval` cut from `d41b4569`.

## 1. The claim, and what is missing

The observation is the user's: one arming on `selective` costs materially more than the same arming on **Adaptive** (`evolutive`). No measurement is on record — the acquisition's slowness is a **parked, unmeasured** todo in [`FEAT_DSC_Route.md`](FEAT_DSC_Route.md), and the engine's timings print only on the device under the `MaroRoute` tag.

The two engines are compositions, not reimplementations: each holds a private [`RouteAvoidEngine`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt) and forwards the seam, so the pipeline, the fine pass, the corner pass, the clock and the flow are **shared**, and the walk differs **by the plan alone**. Everything below is therefore about the plan, the water the plan prices, and the field the plan hands the pull.

## 2. What `selective` does that `evolutive` does not — the per-arm work diff

| Work | `evolutive` | `selective` | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fine-layer extent | one coastal ribbon — the water within `marginM + baseCellM` of the coast | the union of four collars: the shoreline, the band's outer boundary, the zone rims and the depth dilation, 100 m each | [`SelectiveGridPlan.union`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridPlan.kt:173) |
| Fine-layer marking passes | one coast marking | three: the coast collars, the zone-rim ring edges and the depth dilation | [`fineTileGrid`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridBuilder.kt:445) |
| Depth price on the grid | none | a λ-free coefficient written per fine passable cell | [`writeDepthBand`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassGrid.kt:610) |
| Depth price in the search | none | the stored coefficient scaled by λ at read time | [`MultipassSearch`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassSearch.kt:231), `depthK` at [`RoutePassRunner`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePassRunner.kt:96) |
| Depth price in the pull | none | a `withDepthBand` soft source in the guard field, the fine re-tension and the crossing guard | [`costField`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePassPrimitives.kt:129) |
| Depth reads | none | a radial ring scan of 8 bearings out to the band at the fine step — 2 rings (16 reads) at the price band, 5 rings (40 reads) at the collar | [`DepthBandLaw.wallDistanceM`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteFineWater.kt:129) |
| Mask cache | none | `SelectiveMaskCache` holds the pre-snap union keyed on the band width and the four collar widths; the snap-and-merge and the marking rerun per arm | [`SelectiveMaskCache`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteFineWater.kt:183) |

The shared grid is built **once per arm** and the three rungs share it, so the marking work is paid once; the pull, the fine pass and the clock are paid **per rung**.

## 3. The assessment — ranked candidate causes

Each candidate names the mechanism, its code site, the reading that confirms it and the reading that would falsify it. The ranking is provisional: it rests on the code, not on a measurement, and the trace below can reorder it.

### C1 — leading: one zero clearance collapses the price walk's span grouping for the whole field

The pull's price walk proves a span by a single clearance read: where `priceClearanceM(mid) >= halfM`, the whole span is priced from one read; otherwise the span splits, down to one read per interval.

- The depth source is added with **`clearanceAt = { 0.0 }`** — the honest floor the design chose — at [`RoutePassPrimitives.kt:138`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePassPrimitives.kt:138).
- [`RouteCostField.priceClearanceM`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteCostField.kt:306) answers the **minimum** over the soft sources, so a single zero declaration makes the answer `0.0` at **every** point — wherever the band or a zone is the source that would have grouped.
- The walk's own test is [`clearance >= halfM`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassPull.kt:578), which no positive half-length can meet against `0.0`.
- Consequence: on `selective`, the guard field (`RoutePassRunner`), the fine re-tension and the crossing guard (`RouteFinePass`) all carry `withDepthBand = ctx.depthBandActive`, so their price walks degrade to per-interval reads. `evolutive` carries no depth source, so its declarations stay real distances and its spans group.
- **Confirms:** `FINE ... priceReads=` and `marks=` — `selective`'s `priceReads / marks` approaches 1 while `evolutive`'s stays well below it, with the `PULL` · `FINAL` · `FINE` ms scaling with the mark count.
- **Falsifies:** a `priceReads / marks` on `selective` that is not near 1.

### C2 — each ungrouped price read pays the wall-distance ring scan, even in open water

- [`wallDistanceM`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteFineWater.kt:129) scans `DIRECTIONS = 8` bearings at the fine step out to the band width — **16** predicate reads at the price band (45 m at 20 m), **40** at the 100 m collar.
- [`coefAt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteFineWater.kt:146) returns `0.0` beyond the band **only after** the scan, so a read of open water far from any wall still pays the 16.
- The depth source's `priceSec` runs on **every** price read of the guard field, so C2 multiplies C1 rather than adding to it.
- **Confirms:** the price half's own cost — `priceReads × the scan` — visible in the fine pass's `ms` and the pull's price timings.
- **Falsifies:** no fall in `PULL` · `FINAL` ms when the depth price is switched off.

### C3 — the mask marking's own ring scans and the zone-rim pass, once per arm

- The depth-dilation membership runs `wallDistanceM(centre, probe, fineCellM, depthCollarM)` over every grid cell not already a member — up to **40 depth reads each** — at [`MultipassGrid.kt:586`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassGrid.kt:586).
- [`writeDepthBand`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassGrid.kt:610) runs `coefAt` over every passable fine cell — up to 16 reads each, even where the coefficient ends up 0.
- The zone-rim collar adds its own polygon-distance pass, which the design already names as the expensive half of the mask derivation.
- **Confirms:** `GRID layer=fine windows=… cells=… collars=… ms=` for `selective` against `evolutive` on the same pair.
- **Falsifies:** comparable `GRID layer=fine ms` between the two runs.

### C4 — fine-window fragmentation and the fine-priority reading rule

- Four disjoint collars yield more, smaller windows than one contiguous ribbon; the two-layer walk is a chain of windows, and the fine-priority rule makes collar water fine-only, so the walk crosses resolutions repeatedly along the collars.
- **Confirms:** `GRID layer=fine windows=` and the search's `expansions=`.
- **Falsifies:** comparable `windows=` and `expansions=`.

### C5 — extra A\* expansions from the priced band

- The coefficient changes the optimum, so the search may expand more of the priced water to find the cheaper line.
- **Confirms:** `DEVICE PASS … expansions=`.
- **Falsifies:** comparable expansions.

## 3a. Measured on device — 2026-10-08

One device pass under the `MaroRoute` tag: one pair, four arms, every arm grown from the 3704 m corridor to the 7408 m one. The arms were not pace-matched across the whole log, so only the pace-15 kn pair is like-for-like; the log's own `plan=` names the engine.

| Matched run — same pair, corridor 3704 m, pace 15 kn | Adaptive (`evolutive`) | `selective` |
|---|---|---|
| fine-layer build, per build | 0.76 s | 6.0–6.5 s |
| fine-layer builds per arming | 1 | 3 |
| fine cells / windows | 78 431 / 11 | 75 783 / 16 |
| coarse-pull `priceReads / marks` | 0.50–0.58 | 0.94–0.99 |
| `priceMs` per price read | 0.19–0.27 ms | 0.13–0.24 ms |
| A\* expansions, three rungs summed | 22 611 | 47 807 |
| arming, first grid to last rung | ≈15 s | ≈39 s |

- **C3 leads decisively.** The fine build is ~8× dearer **with fewer fine cells than Adaptive**, so the cost is per-cell work — the depth-dilation collar's ring scan, the zone-rim pass and the coefficient written per fine cell — and not the cell count.
- **C1 stands, second.** The coarse pull's grouping collapses: 0.94–0.99 against 0.50–0.58, roughly twice the reads.
- **C2 does not stand.** The reads are twice as many but priced identically per read, so the wall-distance ring scan is not multiplying each read.
- **C4 falls** — windows 16 against 11 with fewer cells is no fragmentation story — and **C5 is minor**, the coarse search only ~1.5× dearer.
- **Still open:** why `selective` builds the base-corridor grid three times where Adaptive builds it once ([`sharedGrid`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:315)'s check-build-assign is unguarded while the rungs run on `Dispatchers.Default`); and one pair cannot split the 8× between the depth dilation and the zone rim.

## 4. The evaluation plan — device first

1. **Run the device trace** — one coastal pair, both engines, the same ends, pace and Driving preference; capture both `MaroRoute` logs (`adb shell setprop log.tag.MaroRoute INFO`, then `adb logcat -s MaroRoute` per run).
2. **Read both logs side by side** and extract, per engine: `CORRIDOR`, `HARVEST`, `GRID layer=coarse ms`, `GRID layer=fine windows/cells/collars ms`, `PULL`/`SNAP`/`FINAL ms`, `FINE points/priceReads/marks/ms`, and `DEVICE PASS` per rung (`plan`, `cellM`, `fineCellM`, cells, `expansions`, path cells, refusals, coarse duration, fine duration), plus `LINE`.
3. **Attribute** the gap to a stage and a factor from the trace alone, against C1–C5 and their stated falsifiers.
4. **Size the harness from the trace** — build the JVM comparison harness only where the trace does not isolate the factor, driving one world and one pair through `RouteAvoidEngine` with `EvolutiveGridPlan` and `SelectiveGridPlan` and printing the same readings.
5. **Isolate each selective-only factor** where a harness is warranted, by toggling it off in turn — the depth price off, each collar width 0, the zone rim off — and measuring the marginal cost.
6. **Write the evaluation** into this feature, fold the attribution into the parked perf todo in [`FEAT_DSC_Route.md`](FEAT_DSC_Route.md), and name the levers without implementing any.

## 5. Self-review report of the assessment

**What holds.** C1 and C2 are read from the code at named lines and are logically forced: a minimum-over-sources clearance of `0.0` cannot prove a span, and `coefAt` cannot return a coefficient without first paying the scan. Neither depends on a measurement to be true as a mechanism.

**What is assumed, not proven.** That the price walk prices enough of the line for C1 to dominate; that `writeDepthBand` runs over the fine grid rather than the corridor; the actual marks per line; and the relative weight of C3 against C1 and C2.

**The strongest counter-argument.** The gap may be **entirely C3**. The mask marking pays 40-read scans over the collar's whole area once per arm, and a coastal pair with a long shallow belt is exactly the shape that inflates it — so a short route over large collars could see the grid build, not the pull, as the cost. The code alone cannot rank C1 against C3; the trace's `GRID layer=fine ms` against `PULL`/`FINE ms` is what decides it.

**The second counter-argument.** The observation may conflate the two engines with a cold cache: the first arm after a settings change pays a `SelectiveMaskCache` miss and the second does not, so the recipe must run each engine twice and read the second run.

**The shape each candidate would leave.** C1/C2 → the pull and fine ms scale with `marks` while `GRID layer=fine ms` stays flat. C3 → `GRID layer=fine ms` dominates and the pull stays flat. C4/C5 → the `windows=` and `expansions=` counts part from `evolutive`'s.

**Named weakness of the plan itself.** It defers the harness until the trace indicts a stage, so an inconclusive trace leaves the session with no second instrument beyond then building one.

## 6. Levers, named and not implemented

- **Uncouple the depth clearance from the field's minimum** — give the depth source its own honest per-point clearance (the raster cell), or exclude it from `priceClearanceM`'s minimum so it cannot collapse the band's and the zones' grouping. The coupling is the part that looks like a defect: one source's declaration silently widening every other source's read count is not declared in the field's KDoc.
- **Precompute or memoise the wall distance** as a field beside the coefficient, so a pull read does not re-scan.
- **Bound the scan by the coefficient** — a coarse depth-cell test before the bearings, and a cheap early-out for points with no blocked sample in the coarse raster.
- **The flat shallow-cell surcharge** the design already names as the fallback, whose clearance is wider and honest.
- **The collar widths, the zone-rim marking cost and the window merge** — the mask side of C3 and C4.
- **The three-rung ladder and the 3704 m corridor halo**, which the parked perf todo already names.

## 7. Open points

- **The device reading ran on 2026-10-08** — four arms under the `MaroRoute` tag, the figures in §3a; the run is the user's own (R97).
- **Whether the depth price earns its cost at all** — `route.selective.depth.priceSecPerM = 0.05` over a 45 m band is the parked todo's own question, and the cost side now has a shape.
- **Whether C1 is a defect rather than a tuning matter** — a soft source's zero clearance widening every other source's read count is a coupling no KDoc declares.

## 8. Proposed solution — a region-anchored, tile-keyed fine layer

The proposal: anchor the fine lattice to a fixed point rather than to the corridor's own box, cache the fine layer region-wide as a keyed tile map that grows lazily as the boat explores, and let one acquisition take only the tiles it needs.

**Verdict — the shape is right, and it is the house pattern one level down.** The depth raster already ships region-wide in `data/app-assets/depth/*.bin` behind a timestamp-keyed cache, so region-scale stamped data sliced on demand is an idiom this codebase already practises.

**Three properties of `MultipassGrid` block it — this is a change, not a cache insert:**

1. **The origin is the corridor's.** [`LatticeFamily.of(corridor, …)`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/WalkLattice.kt:136) derives the lattice origin from the box, so the same water carries different indices per arm. A fixed anchor removes it, and a named constant would serve — but **anchoring on the depth raster's own origin is strictly better**: a fine cell's depth sample becomes an array read rather than the ring scan's bearings, which is the very cost §3a indicts.
2. **The grid is mutable per arm.** The berth carve force-frees the end discs and the rasterizer paints land inside a window — both per-acquisition writes. A cached tile must be immutable, with the carve carried as a copy-on-write overlay on the tiles it touches.
3. **The layout is a boxed `Array<MultipassCell>` beside five `DoubleArray`s**, ≈70–75 B a cell, so a naive 256×256 tile is 4.7 MB. Flattening the cell to a byte tag plus the parallel arrays, and storing a tile **sparsely — only the collar members, not the whole box — is the extraction the proposal names**, and the piece that turns the cache from heavy to cheap.

**Sizes, from the measured cells:** one arming's fine layer is ≈5.5 MB at the 3704 m corridor and ≈10.5 MB at 7408 m; the same collars across a whole region are tens of megabytes, so a tile budget with an LRU is what keeps the map bounded while the coverage keeps growing.

**The win is the marking, not the allocation.** A cached tile skips the ring scans and the zone-rim pass entirely — the ~6 s — so the bytes are the obstacle to clear rather than the goal.

**Staged path, cheapest first:**

1. **Fix the repeated build** — three builds against one is pure waste and needs no architecture: make [`sharedGrid`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:315)'s check-and-build single-flight.
2. **Make the build cheap** — a coarse depth-cell early-out bounding the ring scan, the wall distance precomputed as a field beside the coefficient, and the flat shallow-cell surcharge the design already names.
3. **Flatten the cell** — a `ByteArray` state beside the parallel arrays, roughly halving the per-cell cost before anything is cached.
4. **Then the tile cache** — the fixed anchor, a stamp-keyed tile map, single-flight lazy builds, a byte ceiling with LRU, and the carve as copy-on-write.

**Risks, named.** A fixed anchor touches `LatticeFamily`, the berth carve and the walk's layer indexing; an unbounded map is a leak, so the ceiling and the eviction are part of the design rather than a later add; and the key must carry the depth stamp, the coastline stamp and the EMODnet cutoff, or a re-baked asset leaves stale coefficients cached and the line silently wrong.

## 9. The multi-step plan

Ordered so that every phase is judged on a measurement the one before it made possible, and so the cheap, architecture-free wins land before the structural change. Each phase is one `#implement` run unless it says otherwise.

**P0 — The instrument, the harness and the baseline.** The harness's job is to make P1–P4 measurable without the device; the device pass stays the acceptance, not the instrument. P0 is the only phase that touches shipped code before P1, and it does so once — the instrument seam.

- P0.0 **The instrument seam** — every reading the harness needs rides [`trace()`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:657) into `Log.i`, which [`logEnabled`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:225) keeps inert on the JVM by design, so the harness cannot read them as things stand. Give [`RouteAvoidEngine`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:126) an optional trace sink — default `null`, the gated `Log.i` path — and route `trace()` through it, so a JVM caller captures every line and the device behaviour is unchanged; the three seats already take a `trace: (() -> String) -> Unit`, so the sink threads through them untouched.
- P0.1 **The fixture world** — a `MultipassWorld` fake holding a coast with a shallow depth belt and one speed zone, sized so both plans' collars are non-trivial, with `AppConfig` loaded from `maro.properties` so the `route.selective.*` collar widths and the `route.*.grid.*` metres are live; the zone makes the zone-rim collar — the third of `selective`'s four — non-empty.
- P0.2 **The harness** — drive the **full three-rung ladder** (all three `startLookup` calls, not one rung) through `RouteAvoidEngine` with `EvolutiveGridPlan` and `SelectiveGridPlan`, capture the sink, and run each engine **twice**, recording the second run: the `SelectiveMaskCache` is process-static and the JVM needs warm-up.
- P0.3 **The baseline record** — the harness prints the same key=value tokens the device trace prints (`GRID … ms`, `PULL`/`FINAL priceReads/marks/priceMs`, `FINE …`, `DEVICE PASS … expansions`) plus a build count per arming, under a JUnit `@Test` writing to stdout (the repo's `test-*.log` habit), so P1–P4 diff against one fixed vocabulary.
- P0.4 **The gate** — the harness must reproduce the measured **structural** ranking, not the device's wall-clock: `priceReads/marks` ≈ 1 against ≈ 0.5, and the fine build dominating the pull. The build count is **not** plan-dependent — one [`sharedGrid`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:315) path serves both engines, so a genuinely concurrent ladder builds three times on **either** plan and a serialised one builds once; the device's 3-vs-1 was a scheduling outcome, and P1.1 is what removes it. Absolute ms is secondary, or JVM noise fails a correct fixture.

**P0 — landed 2026-10-08.** The harness is [`RouteSelectivePerfEvalTest.kt`](../../app/src/test/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteSelectivePerfEvalTest.kt:1) over the `BeltWorld` fixture; the warm run read `priceReads/marks` **0.9989** on `selective` against **0.5583** on `evolutive`, and the fine build **134.7 ms** on `selective` against **18.4 ms** on `evolutive` — both matching §3a, the ~8× on fewer fine cells among them. The gate pinned `fineBuilds == 3` as the pre-P1.1 state; **P1.1 has since flipped it to `== 1`** (see below). Three review notes stood, none a blocker: the worker count behind `Dispatchers.Default` was unpinned, which P1.2's latch retirement has since neutralised; nothing asserts the collars stay non-trivial, so a future degenerate mask could pass silently; and the record prints through the JUnit XML's `system-out`, since [`build.gradle.kts`](../../app/build.gradle.kts:180) sets no `showStandardStreams`.

**P1 — Stop the repeated build.** No architecture: the three rungs race on [`sharedGrid`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:325)'s unguarded check-build-assign, so a concurrent arm rasterises the corridor once per rung. That race is the whole of P1 — a rung that finds no path or a forced crossing re-rasterises at the doubled reach on purpose ([`searchRung`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:359)), and the growth path is out of scope.

- P1.1 **Single-flight the twin, per arm** — the naive `Mutex.withLock` fix trips on one fact: [`routesToCompute`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:239) is not `suspend`, so a `Mutex` cannot wrap its `ladderGrid = null` reset. Give the ladder a **per-arm holder** — its own `Mutex` plus the `Deferred` — created in `routesToCompute` and published with one write, and make [`sharedGrid`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:325) `suspend` so the first rung computes the `Deferred` **inside** the holder's lock and the other two await it, so no rung starts a build of its own and a fresh arm cannot be clobbered by a stale one. The shared grid keeps the first caller's `world` and `pace`, which the world's own between-solves reload contract makes safe.
- P1.2 **Flip the P0 gate, not a new fixture** — the harness already drives three concurrent `startLookup` calls and counts `GRID layer=fine`; change its `fineBuilds` expectation from `== 3` to `== 1` for both plans in [`RouteSelectivePerfEvalTest.kt`](../../app/src/test/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteSelectivePerfEvalTest.kt:229). Post-P1.1 the count no longer turns on `Dispatchers.Default`'s width, so retire the start-alignment latch [`alignRungs`](../../app/src/test/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteSelectivePerfEvalTest.kt:137) with it, clearing the ≤2-worker flakiness the P0 review flagged. The `== 1` is fixture-conditioned on an un-grown reach; growth is the one thing that would move it.
- P1.3 Read the mechanism on device: `CORRIDOR` and `GRID layer=fine` print once per arm at an un-grown reach — both are emitted once per [`buildGrid`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridBuilder.kt:35), so their count is the build count.

**P1 — landed 2026-10-08.** [`RouteAvoidEngine.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:198) carries a per-arm [`LadderGridHolder`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:820) — its own `Mutex` plus the `Deferred` — created in [`routesToCompute`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:259) and published with one write; [`sharedGrid`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:341) is `suspend` and builds inside the holder's lock while the other two rungs await the same `Deferred`, and the holder is captured at [`startLookup`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:276) and threaded through [`runComputation`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt:290). The harness gate flipped to `fineBuilds == 1` on both plans and the start-alignment latch was retired ([`RouteSelectivePerfEvalTest.kt`](../../app/src/test/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteSelectivePerfEvalTest.kt:206)); `apk-build.bat` and the full suite are green. The warm run now reads a single fine build per arm — **26.4 ms** on `selective` against **2.9 ms** on `evolutive` — with the price density unmoved. One behaviour delta, judged safe: the shared grid keeps the **first** rung's `world` and `pace`, embedded in its base cost, so a mid-arm pace move can no longer let the rungs diverge. Four non-blocking review nits stand: an unreachable initial holder; the shared build survives a lookup cancel; `repairedOrigin`/`repairedDestination` are still un-captured; and `ladderHolder` is non-volatile, safe only because it is written and read on one thread.

**P2 — Make the build's reads cheap.** The fine build is the selective-only hot spot — **26.4 ms** against `evolutive`'s **2.9 ms** in the fixture, ≈9× on fewer cells — and the cost is the per-cell radial scan both depth passes pay: [`DepthBandLaw.wallDistanceM`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteFineWater.kt:129) fires 8 bearings × rings, ~40 `world.depthAt` probes a cell at the 100 m collar ([`applyFineMask`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassGrid.kt:586)) and ~16–24 at the band ([`writeDepthBand`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassGrid.kt:610), which sees only the collar's passable cells because the mask runs first). **Two constraints bind every lever.** The probe is a live `(LatLng) -> Boolean` over the depth raster, so there is **no coarse blocked raster** to prefilter on — the only prefilter available is the gate's own blocked cells, or a distance field built over them. And the coefficient the rasterizer writes and the one the pull's guard computes must stay **one function of the point**, or the search and the guard price the same water differently: an early-out must therefore be **conservative** — it may skip only a cell it can prove is beyond the width, falling back to the scan otherwise — while a precomputed field is a **value change** that must move the guard with it.

- P2.1 **Split the cost first** — the harness already prints the fine build; extend it to toggle the depth collar, the band write and the zone rim in turn, so the 9× lands on a pass — the membership ([`MultipassGrid.kt:555`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassGrid.kt:555)), the coefficient write ([`writeDepthBand`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassGrid.kt:638)) or the zone-rim walk ([`MultipassGrid.kt:591`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassGrid.kt:591)) — and no lever is applied on a guess.
- P2.2 **A conservative early-out for the collar membership** — collect the grid's gate-blocked cells once and skip the bearing scan for any cell whose nearest blocked cell is provably beyond `depthCollarM`, falling back to [`wallDistanceM`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteFineWater.kt:129) otherwise. The bound reasons over blocked **cell centres** while the law samples **points**, so it is exact only where the wall is at least a fine cell thick; every other cell takes the law's own scan, so the membership is the law's own and unmoved on the measured fixture.
