# Feature: Route

State doc — what the mode is, what the code does today, and what is left. Present tense only: the
`## Implemented` log, the closed walk levels and the struck requirements were removed on the user's word
of 2026-10-07, so this file states the current code and nothing else.

## Concept

Set a destination and have the app draw the route from the boat's position to it, then save that line as
an ordinary track.

- **The two ends are the drawer's** — the hamburger menu's Route sub-section under Navigation holds one dropdown per end, persisted per navigation mode; the start offers the current position, the marker position and every marker flagged as an origin, the destination the marker position and every marker flagged as a destination.
- **An end is read when the action is pressed** — where either end is the current position or the marker position, its value is the corresponding point **at the time of the route action**, and nothing re-reads it afterwards.
- **Arming computes at once** — the drawer's action and the map's route fan, whose `Route` and `Route (auto)` children open the same trigger on the pair that stands when they are pressed, are the doors; the fan's `Follow` child takes the settled or partial line once it stands, and the fan's parent only opens the arc. Nothing re-asks afterwards, so one arming holds one answer and no line is ever replaced from inside the mode — with one exception, the **loss mirror** (R99): while a route is followed its two ends reverse **in place** when the reading loses ground against its own recent low, the drawn line and its clock untouched and no search re-run. **The drawer's Go is the one door that restarts** (R101): it is a true restart from any state and the only arming that ends a standing mode first.
- **The engine offers three routes at once** — the ladder computes three fixed aversions (around · best · fast) as separate lookups over one shared grid, each row printing its own distance and ETA, and the engine crowns the lowest score among the rungs that have landed (R98). **Each rung's λ is a key of its own** — `route.avoid.ladder.around|best|fast.lambda`, read through `AppConfig` and published by `RoutePreference`, which now holds the three rungs' λ alone — and the **crown is one sum over the three drawn lines**: the `Slow water` dial states how many minutes a minute in slow water counts as (**Fastest** · 1x · 2x · 3x · 4x · **Ignore time**), and the ranking crowns the lowest trip time plus that weight times the line's own seconds under a limit.
- **The drawing is the selected line, reinforced by shape and opacity** — the selected line is drawn at the route's own transparency during the acquisition and once confirmed, with a derived under-stroke beneath it (R93), and every line beside it wears the one shared dimming key; while followed the line splits at the boat, so the run behind wears that same dimming key and the run ahead stays at full strength under the edge; **at the destination nothing is left ahead** — fewer than two points is zero points — so the covered line alone stands, in that same dimming key. **No ladder of superseded lines exists.**
- **The acquisition owns three outcomes** — `Save to track` (the selected line, greyed once written), `Select route` (navigation on it, the other routes dropped) and the red `Discard route` (leaves through the deferred discard, R92). Once selected there is no panel at all: the ordinary dashboard returns, and the mode's presence is the line, the toggle and the one exit dialog — `Save and Leave` · `Continue` · `Discard route` while the line is unwritten, the save door disabled and the third reading `Leave` once it is a track (R59, R100). That third door is **two-state like the dialog's own**: while the selected line is unwritten it reads the acquisition's own short `Discard` / `Abandonner` (`route_acq_discard`) — it holds a third of the weighted footer row, where the dialog's longer loss word would not fit — and once the line is a track it hands over to the exit dialog's own `Leave` / `Quitter`, accent and not red, derived from `routeExitDoors(written = true).loss`, its word following the cost, never the surface. The word moves, the act does not: every ending of the panel and of the dialog leaves through the **deferred window** (R92), raising `route_left_toast` — *Route left* / *Route quittée*.
- **Speeds come from where the line is** — in open water the pace is the free-water pace setting — its first stop, `Any`, planning at the boat's own pace and falling back to the shipped value until the boat has evidence; at a point inside the 300 m band or a regulated speed zone the speed is that limit, the strictest one where both hold, and the clock follows the limit in force. The band's own limit is read **whatever its price switch says**: `route.avoid.zone300.enabled` prices water, it never suspends a limit. The **dummy's fixed 15 kn** is the placeholder's own fiction and the one place this does not hold.
- **An end may need moving** — an origin or destination on land, on water shallower than the minimum depth, or within `route.repair.clearanceM` (25 m) of the coast or the measured 3 m line is **moved to the nearest clear water**, never across land or a shoal, and the route panel says so for either end (2026-10-20); where no clear point stands close by, the nearest point clear of the gate's wall and then the old first-water rule take it, so no end routed before is refused.
- **A saved route is an ordinary track carrying the `route` flag** — listed, exported, drawn and deleted like any other.
- **While a route is followed the distance tile becomes the route's own** — its **main figure is the time left**, the bare ETA in force, over one bottom line carrying the distance left, the `Slow water` dial's own word and the cruising pace (`route_trip_line_fmt`). The plan's age is not shown, and neither is a forced-crossing note: this cell reads the trip and nothing else. The two are **one cell the boat can flip**: a tap on the trip tile returns it to the shore reading and a tap there brings the trip back, a freshly armed route opening on the trip face — and with no route the cell is the ordinary distance tile, untappable. The cell wears a **border pulsing in the route toggle's own blue** (`AppConfig.routeNavigateColor`), beating on the app's single pulse home; the border marks the followed route rather than one face, so the trip reading and the shore reading alike wear it and one beat spans the flip. The trip cell and the drawer band alike read `remainingFrom(boat)` off **whatever orientation the plan stands in** (R99), so the loss mirror's ETA and distance face the new destination with no second arithmetic.

## Current state of the code

A third row, `selective`, was **retired on 2026-10-16** with its four collars, its depth price and its keys ([`261016_FEAT_PLN_Route_selective-retirement.md`](xxArchive/261016_FEAT_PLN_Route_selective-retirement.md)), so the depth wall and the margin stay the shared layer's for both rows that remain. `route.engine.id` names the shipped one and an unclaimed id falls back to it.
- **The ladder is drawn, and the crown is one sum** (R98) — [`RoutePreference.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePreference.kt:1) holds the three rungs' λ values alone, each read from its own ladder key; [`RoutePassRanking.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePassRanking.kt:1) crowns the landed rung with the lowest score — the trip time plus the `Slow water` dial's weight times the line's own seconds under a limit, where a `null` weight is the **ignore time** stop weighing those seconds alone with the clock as its tie, and a difference under `route.avoid.ladder.tieToleranceSec` leaves the crown where it stands; and the engine folds that at every rung's terminal into the **running best** the update carries. Untouched, the seat follows that winner; **a hand pick holds through the search** (re-found by lookup id across a fold) and **the last landing moves the seat to the top row**, overriding the pick and raising `route_best_selected_toast` when it moved one; the fan's `Route auto` child takes the final best.
- **The shared layer every engine stands on is `multipass`** — tagged costed cells, an 8-neighbour A\*, a source-parameterized taut pull, the ends' discs, one cost field whose base is always set and whose sources only add, the racing-line corner pass, the clock that obeys the limit in force, the tangent corners, the zone geometry and the four pass seats ([`spatial/multipass/`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass)). **The plan seam sits over it** ([`RouteGridPlan.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridPlan.kt:30)): each engine's own walk cell and fine cell are its plan's decision, never the pipeline's, and the seam also says **whether its water pays the gate berth** (`pricesBerth`) — `false` for `avoid`, `true` for `evolutive`, whose berth law then also stands the gate's wall at its floor. **A two-layer walk reads the fine cell over the coarse copy** — where a passable fine cell stands over the same water as a coarse cell, the fine cell is the cell the search reads and the coarse copy is not read, while land on the fine grid leaves the coarse cell its ordinary role; so `evolutive`'s coastal ribbon is fine-only water and its line and clock follow it, and `avoid`'s single-grid walk is untouched.
- **The fine layer is cached as pace-free tiles on the anchored lattice, behind an on-disk store** — [`FineTile.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTile.kt) holds a tile's collar members sparsely, its one price-bearing double a **pace-free per-member factor** (the base is re-applied once, from the target grid's own base, when the tile is materialised), so one tile serves every speed; [`TileKey.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/TileKey.kt) keys it on every value its rasterisation reads **but the pace**, [`FineTileCodec.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTileCodec.kt) writes a key-verified entry, the store keeping one installed build's entries only (no format version, 2026-10-20), [`DirectoryFineTileStore.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/DirectoryFineTileStore.kt) persists them on disk behind the `route.fineTile.maxBytes` ceiling — 96 MiB, and a **soft** one: the warm-up's home area rides a `protect` set, so the store reclaims its **least recently used unprotected** tiles from one **access-ordered index** — no save lists or sorts the directory — takes no prepared home tile, and reports once when only protected bytes are left above it, while `list()` keeps reading the directory because the warm-up treats a name as proof a file stands — the app edge raising that first reclamation as a toast **and** pushing it as a notification on the app's single channel, behind one `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` ask at startup — and [`FineTileMap.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTileMap.kt) builds each once behind a byte ceiling with LRU eviction and an in-flight guard, counts a store-served lookup a hit and writes each build back — so a per-arm fine window is assembled by copying the tiles that cover it, the arm's own grid takes the end discs, and a second arming marks only the tiles a farther route newly needs.
- **A startup warm-up fills those tiles before an arm asks for them, then grows outward until the phone or the map says stop** — [`FineTilePrewarm.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTilePrewarm.kt) walks the plan's own tiles through the **builder's own key and build** ([`RouteGridBuilder.fineTilesOver`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridBuilder.kt:458), the arm's `fineTileKey` and `buildFineTile`), so a tile it makes is the very tile an arm over that water asks for — never a second derivation. It is **store-first** — a warm tile is skipped by name before any build, so a fully warm ring builds nothing and opens no window at all — and ordered **own-ship outward** from the last known position (the seed area's centre as fallback). [`FineTileWarm.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTileWarm.kt) seeds it on that area (`route.prewarm.box`, the Cagnes-sur-Mer → Cannes coast) and takes that seed ring **handed in** by the app edge, which derives it once and protects its entry names before the walk's first fullness check — so protection never depends on a stop — and grows the area **outward in rings** while the store has room, stopping on the store's own **room under the target share of its ceiling** ([`TileStore.fullness()`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/TileStore.kt) read through `StoreFullness.hasRoomAt`, so a live verdict that cannot latch and the rest of the ceiling stands as a reserve for water the seed area does not cover), on the pass's own pack cap, or on the map's baked bounds — a box that falls outside the region is reported rather than shrunk, **no radius dial exists**, and the walk's end line carries the map's hit rate.
- **Its workplace is its own** — the pass runs at the app edge ([`MapRoutePrewarmEffect`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/MapRouteEffects.kt)) on a **worker of its own at a background thread priority**, off the screen's thread, and it starts once both baked layers can answer rather than on the boat being idle: a tile built before the depth grid loads would key `depthGateEnabled = false` and no arm could ever reach it. It **yields** to an armed route and to a depth-raster regeneration, both re-asked per tile and per ring, and it **stands down** on battery-saver, re-read at every ask; one process-level slot holds one walk, so an Activity recreation's successor waits rather than starting a second.
- **Its white dot is the warm-up's own `busy` window**, opened for each tile the walk really builds and merged across them by the holder's grace (`FineTileCache.beginWarmTile` / `endWarmTile`), so the routing square's mark stands for the walk's work rather than flickering across its tiles, goes **dark the moment the walk waits at its gate** — an armed route, a competing heavy job or battery-saver — and lights nothing at all where every ring is already warm. The map reports no per-lookup activity, so an arm's own store reads never light it.
- **The store answers how full it is, live, and under which share** — [`TileStore.fullness()`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/TileStore.kt) hands the bytes held, the ceiling and **the size of the entry it wrote last**, and [`StoreFullness.hasRoomAt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/TileStore.kt) answers whether one more entry of that size still fits **under a share of the ceiling** — the `route.prewarm.targetPct` the warm-up holds its growth to, so the rest of the ceiling stands as a reserve for water the seed area does not cover; `StoreFullness.atCeiling` is that question asked in full. It is recomputed on every read rather than latched, because a save trims back to the ceiling and a bare `held >= ceiling` test would never be observable; the storeless default answers **unknown**, so a fixture with no store behaves exactly as it did before the seam existed.

- **The corner pass rounds a line's bends from what the line gives it** — the fit is a clothoid → arc → clothoid whose reach takes the whole **significant** leg, a kink too flat to be a corner keeping its own point and taking no room, and the corner floor is a **target rather than a refusal**: the room may draw a bend under it, down to the pass's own 1 kn bottom. A close pair of bends turning the **same way** within the tangent a pace-speed curve needs for their summed angle is read as **one turn**, over the crossing of its two outer legs; two bends on one leg **share** it, each weighted by its own angle; and the overlap guard measures bow to bow, a bow taking back the points it has passed on either side so the drawn line never folds back. The coast test is **relative to the corner it replaces** — a bow may not come closer to the shore than the corner itself — and so is the depth gate's, at its preferred standoff, the blocked water itself staying absolute and a point outside the ends' waiver never under the wall; every straight the pass draws that no earlier stage measured — a bow's chords, the chords joining it past the kinks it passes, a merged pair's extensions to their crossing — is measured against the same walls; and every bend's answer, its room, its share, the limit in force at it and whether it stands in the band are named by the `CORNERS` and `CORNER` lines the pass prints on the `MaroRoute` trace. Design, measurements and the device evidence live in [`261013_FEAT_PLN_Route_corner-pass-fixes.md`](261013_FEAT_PLN_Route_corner-pass-fixes.md); [`RouteCornerPassTest`](../../app/src/test/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteCornerPassTest.kt) pins each rule.

- **A pair arms as one line from either end, within the bar** — three changes, measured over the baked region by the gated `RouteDirectionSymmetryTest` over `BakedRegionWorld` (`-Dmaro.prebake=true`): the pass's **search leaves the end that sorts first** by latitude, then longitude, the cells and the guard's points swapped together and the path turned back, walking the other way once if that lands no path ([`RoutePassRunner.searchFromCanonicalEnd`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePassRunner.kt), the rule read from `sortsFirst`); the two pulls run **both ways** and keep the cheaper line, priced in a canonical direction ([`MultipassPull.pullBothWays`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/MultipassPull.kt)); and a coarse block's fine copy has its **step back out** ([`WalkWindows.coarseNeighbourOfCopy`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/WalkLattice.kt)). The 24-pair survey went from 5 plan-pair runs over the bar (same crown, 100 m, 30 s) to 0, the line gap to a worst 0.4 m. **It costs:** the two-sided pull doubles the pull, which is 75–90 % of the coarse pass, so a ladder takes 64 % (`avoid`) to 79 % (`evolutive`) longer on a desktop JVM, and a pair with no path walks up to four searches instead of two. Record: [`261020_FEAT_PLN_Route_direction-symmetry.md`](261020_FEAT_PLN_Route_direction-symmetry.md).

### Placement and the three seams

The route sits in the app's own slicing rather than in a package of its own, and the isolation survives as three seams on shared files:

1. `MapScreen.kt` — the engine resolved at one expression, one `RouteHost(...)` call, the panel composed into the dashboard slot, the exit doors.
2. The drawer — the Route sub-section and the read-only mode summary, both inside the Navigation card.
3. The Settings overlay — the engine's row, the free-water pace, the **Slow water** dial and the **Active route** colour, held by `SettingsManager`.

### The mode's flow

```text
Idle → a door armed on the standing pair → Choosing (the search runs; the panel and the provisional line)
Choosing → Select route → Following (the line, the toggle, the one exit dialog)
Choosing → Save to track / Discard route → Idle
Following → Save to track → Following (the followed line is written; the mode stays)
Following → the toggle's off or the back key → the one dialog → save and exit · continue · discard
Any state → the drawer's Go → Choosing afresh (an unwritten followed line asks first, R101)
```

The save door serves the **followed** line too (2026-10-17): the fan's `Save` child writes the line being
followed, gated on that line being unwritten — a followed saved track is already written, and an early
save's draft is finalised over the followed line when the mode starts following it, so the door never
writes a second track for the same line. The link is read through one helper for both lines
([`routeLinkSaved`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt:516)), which bridges
the loss mirror's reversal, and a write already in flight counts as written and refuses a second press.

A discard is **two-phase** (R92): the display ends at once and the disposal waits on the undo toast.

Arrival is a cue and no state (R100): when the time-to-go falls through the arrival threshold the exit
dialog is raised under a *reached your destination* prompt, and the trip cell still reads zero while the
line stays drawn.

### Decoupling rules

1. Route depends only on `data/model`'s `LatLng`, `RoutePoint` and `RouteResult`, the shared `spatial/Units.kt` and `SpatialOperations`, the `RouteEngine` seam, and the shared `ui.color.reinforcedColor` helper (R93).
2. An engine that wants the water reads it through **its own** world interface; the dummy reads none at all.
3. The feature declares no conversion constant, no baked artifact and no dependency of its own, and the zone and band values stay the RegulatedZones layer's.
4. All Route runtime state lives in `RouteViewModel` (`StateFlow`), and `RouteHost` is the only file touching osmdroid.

- **R91 — The route fan closes on a child press only for follow and acquire** — **`Route (auto)` is also live while a search runs** (2026-10-19): the press keeps that search, only sets the auto-follow intent (the winner is followed when the last route lands) and raises `route_auto_follow_toast`; the cell is off once the search has ended, an early select is committed or the intent stands ([`routeAutoChildEnabled`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt)). Pressing a child fires that child's action; the **follow**, **acquire** (`Route`) and **auto** (`Route auto`) children also close the arc, while **Discard** and **Save** leave it expanded, and otherwise the fan closes on the back key, a tap on the scrim, or the parent anchor's own toggle, the sibling fan's faded anchor being a known limit aside.
- **R92 — Every route ending is two-phase: the display ends at once, the disposal waits on its toast** — an ending press — the panel's `Discard route` while the selected line is unwritten and its `Leave` once it is a track, the fan's Discard, the toggle-off and the back key inside the acquisition, and both of the exit dialog's endings, its third door in either state (`Discard route` while the press would throw the line away, `Leave` once it cannot) and its `Save and Leave` door, which writes the track first — sets a `pendingDiscard` window instead of disposing: the acquisition panel and the route line leave and the toggle reads off, while `routeArmed`/`routeState` stay live and the engine keeps running underneath. The toast names what the press cost — `route_discard_acq_toast` or `route_discard_followed_toast` for a discard, `route_left_toast` for a leave. The real disposal — `endRouteMode()` plus the engine's `cancelLookup` — runs when the toast is dismissed: its timeout, a horizontal swipe, the second back press, or **New...**; **Undo** clears the window and the panel, line, toggle and selection return with nothing recomputed. **A new arming commits then arms**, the fan's Save/Follow act on the live mode and supersede the window, and the route toast jumps the snackbar queue. Every explicit arming passes the force-fresh flag, so the acquisition always searches the standing ends afresh.
- **R93 — The selected route is reinforced by shape and opacity** — a **derived edge** is drawn beneath the selected line in both phases — the selected rung during the acquisition and the followed line while `Following` — its colour the line's own pushed toward black by the one lever `ui.reinforce.darkenPct` (read through `AppConfig.uiReinforceDarkenPct`, clamped 0..100) and its width the key `route.line.casing.widthDp`, reusing the core width's own `1f / 3f..24f` bounds; the edge takes the line's own transparency, so **no colour, brightness or alpha key** is added. `reinforcedColor` is pure and RGB-only with the **caller** reading the lever. While `Following`, slot 0 draws the split's remaining run alone — never the whole line beneath the travelled overlay — the casing mirrors exactly what slot 0 draws, and `route_travelled` carries the covered run whenever it is a real run; **where fewer than two points remain the run is empty** — zero points is nothing ahead — so slot 0 and its casing stand down and the travelled run alone draws the covered line in the shared dimming, the paint's one face rule. The word **arrival** is the cue's alone (R100): this paint case is an empty remaining run, never a state. `RoutePlan.splitAt(from)` splits the line at the boat — the travelled run wearing `route.dimmed.transparencyPct` and the remaining run at full strength under the edge — and `remainingFrom` reads the same projection, so it has one home. **The read direction is not an identity** (R99): the split, the travelled run, the casing and the destination pin are all read off the plan object, so a mirrored plan repaints them in the same stroke and nothing re-searches.
- **R94 — The seat never parks on an empty row** — [`routeSeatedIndex(hasPlan, preferredIndex)`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt:247) answers the preferred page when it holds a plan, otherwise the page **holding a plan** nearest the preferred one (the lower index winning an even split), otherwise the preferred index unchanged, so no state survives in which the selection holds nothing while a line stands beside it. Arming seats the **main's own page**, the dial naming no rung. The seat is `RouteViewModel.selectedIndex`, while `RouteState.Choosing.plan` is the **main's** plan (index 0).
- **R95 — The rungs are named for what their lines do, presented by ETA, and the dial's own sum crowns them (R98)** — the three rungs read **Detour · Balanced · Coastal** (`Détour · Équilibré · Côtier`, `route_rung_around|balanced|through`), words that name the line rather than a preference; the acquisition presents them by rank once landed, the rest in the ladder's natural order, while the provisional line stays on the main; the three λ alone separate the rungs, and the `Slow water` row's own words carry the crown's weight in both locales (**Fastest** · 1x · 2x · 3x · 4x · **Ignore time**).
- **R96 — A rung's row carries a provisional distance and ETA from its cell path on, replaced twice** — a first pair is taken **at the search → pull boundary** from the raw cell path (its length and one enforced-limit read, [`timeLineWithLimits`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteEta.kt:73)) — an overestimate by nature, the user accepting the jump — and replaced at the **pull → snap boundary** by the pulled line's. It rides the boundary update that already carries the line ([`RouteUpdate.provisional`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteEngine.kt:191)); **every rung narrates its own stage chain**, so each row prints its own stage word. The row holds it per page — `RoutePage.provisional`, keyed by the lookup/computation id — and **clears it when that rung's terminal update lands**. **The provisional clock is the enforced-limit clock; the settled time is the profile clock.**
- **R97 — The device pass is evidence, not a build order** — a pass has two jobs. As an **acceptance** it shows an optimisation changed nothing the user sees — the same line, distance and clock — where the unit suite only proves arithmetic on fixtures. As a **measurement** it prints the figure that prices the saving. It is the **user's own** and the agent never touches the device. It is **not a precondition for a code landing** — every change is built and judged on fixtures and the unit suite — and only the steps whose subject *is* a reading wait on it.
- **R98 — One dial weighs the crown, a slow second is weighted by its own limit, and the panel shows the order** — the `Slow water` row (`route.avoid.slowWater.weight`, seeded 4, one persisted stop index) states how many minutes a minute in slow water counts as: **Fastest** (weight 0) takes the quickest trip, 1x–4x add that weight times the line's **weighted** seconds under a limit, and **Ignore time** weighs those seconds alone with the clock as its tie. Each second's weight is **linear in its own limit's shortfall** — `(pace − limit) / pace`, floored at zero, so 0.8 at 5 kn and 0.2 at 20 kn on a 25 kn pace and **nothing at or above the pace** — defined once in `slowWaterSeverity()`; the seconds measured are a ring's interior, the 300 m band and the ramps that serve a limit, a bend's own floor outside them, and a leg meeting **no** limit weighs **nothing**. The published per-limit table stays **plain seconds**, so the panel's minutes, the drawing, the clock and the dial are unmoved, while the crown's figure becomes the line's own **excess under its limits** — the quantity the drawing bills. The engine folds the rungs that have landed through [`RoutePassRanking`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePassRanking.kt:1) at **every rung's terminal**, riding the terminal update as `RouteRunningBest` while it computes and as `RouteVerdict` once the set has settled — the crown and every landed rung's **rank**, distinct `1..n`: `route.avoid.ladder.tieToleranceSec` (30 s) decides what counts as a tie and the crown stays that group's head, while the order inside a group runs the **exact score**, then **fewer weighted seconds**, then the **shorter trip**, then the row that **landed first**. The seat **follows the running best on every improvement** until a hand touch, which **holds the pick through the search**; **the last landing moves the seat to the top row**, overriding the pick, with a toast when it moved one; arming seats the main's page, and the fan's `Route auto` child takes the **final best** once the set has settled. The panel puts each landed row's **rank** on its second line — live over the rungs landed so far, a merged twin counted with its survivor and the ranks dense `1..k` — and closes the **crowned** row's name with a checkmark in the route accent only after the last rung, while the selection keeps the taken-choice face; every row prints **time first, distance second**.
- **R99 — A followed route loses ground and mirrors its two ends in place** — while `Following`, the reading **toggles** once the trip figure's own time-to-go (`remainingFrom(boat).durationSec`) is `route.follow.swap.lossSec` worse than its own recent low; a fall or a flat reading is the ordinary progressing state and does nothing, and the toggle is **never gated by the arrival threshold** — what keeps it quiet at the destination is the number's own flatness, clamped to the route's end. The low is the smallest sample inside a look-back of **twice the loss** (derived, not a dial), so the route turns the moment the loss accrues while a drift slower than half the route's own pace never counts; the history is bucketed to about one a second on `SystemClock` elapsed-realtime, **cleared at every flip** — the jump to the other end's value belongs to the other orientation — and on entering `Following`, with `route.follow.swap.debounceSec` spacing the toggles. The predicate is one pure function, [`routeEtaToggle`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt), with [`routeEtaStep`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt) advancing the history, and the mirror one plan method, [`RoutePlan.reversed`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteViewModel.kt). It is **in place only**: `points` and `legTimesSec` reverse and the two ends exchange while every settled total rides unchanged, so the drawn geometry and the clock stay byte-identical and no lookup runs — [`RouteViewModel.onBoatFix`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteViewModel.kt) is fed the map's own fix and nothing re-arms. The route keeps its **identity**: the session's route-to-track link, `isRouteSaved` and the persisted end ids (R82) do not fork with the read direction, so a mirrored saved route lists and reopens as the same route.
- **R100 — The arrival cue rides the exit dialog's own doors** — when `remainingFrom(boat).durationSec` falls through `route.follow.arrival.etaSec` while still closing, **as a new look-back low**, the mode raises the exit dialog ([`MapDialogHost`](app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/MapDialogHost.kt)) under a title-only *reached your destination* prompt (`route_arrival_title`, no message), with the same three doors at every state, decided by one axis — **is this line already a track?**: while the line is **unwritten** *Save route and exit* (accent, enabled) · *Continue* (secondary) · *Discard route* (red); once it is **written** the save door keeps its place and is **disabled** · *Continue* (secondary) · *Leave* (the accent, and **not red**, because nothing is lost) — the third door's word following the **cost** rather than the raiser. The cue is **armed on entering `Following` from above the threshold, and armed again by every flip** — so a route taken up already inside the zone raises no prompt at the outset, while the mirrored end behaves exactly like the first and both ends of the route ask — and it **disarms on firing and re-arms whenever the time-to-go reads back above the threshold**, so a boat that leaves and comes back down, or turns for the other end, legitimately re-prompts: the same loss-versus-low test decides the flip and the prompt alike, and no second dial is invented. The doors that keep the route — *Continue* and a dismissal (no action) — leave the mode `Following`, while the doors that end it make the flip moot. **A reversal outranks the prompt**: if the loss accrues while the cue is open, the cue is closed as a **no action** dismissal and the flip proceeds. One surface, two raisers: a raise while the dialog stands is that one dialog with the user's own title, and the arrival raiser never stacks a second.

- **R101 — The menu's Go restarts the search from any state** — [`goRoute`](app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/MapScreen.kt) settles a standing discard window first, ignores the press while a route write is in flight (that write reads the mode's armed ids, which an ending would clear), refuses a pair under `route.min.acquisition.lengthM` **before** anything ends, and then restarts: an idle press arms as before, a search or a followed route that is already a track ends through `endRouteMode()` and arms afresh with no question, and a followed line **not yet written** raises the exit dialog under its `RESTART` reason — the same three doors, labelled *Save and restart* · *Keep following* · *Discard route* — whose endings hand over to a fresh arming with no deferred window; *Save and restart* arms only after the write has landed. Every arming that took raises `route_started_toast` on the map's notice banner (`routeNoticeToast`, once the refusal toast). The map square, the fan's children and the snack's *New* action still arm through `armRouteMode` untouched.

## Rules

- **The pull straightens by dropping points and judges a chord on total time** (2026-10-17): `straightenLine` (Douglas–Peucker, 10 m) runs after the pull and before the corner pass, and every chord proof — the price refusal, the sweep's triple and window, `chordLegal` — compares travel at the pace plus soft price, never soft price alone; the wall is untouched. Decision and open points in [`FEAT_DOC_Route_decisions.md`](FEAT_DOC_Route_decisions.md) §8.
- **The user-facing word is *route search*, never *acquisition*** (2026-10-19): the panel title, the status (*Searching*) and the fan's `Route` child read it (the snack's action is the bare *New* / *Nouvelle*, for room), while the code, the plans and this file keep *acquisition* as internal vocabulary. French says *route* throughout the app, never *itinéraire*. A button carries the noun only where its surface does not already name the route: the exit dialog's destructive door reads *Discard route*, the panel's *Discard*, and the fan's icon-only children each name the route (`Route search`, `Save route`, `Follow route`, `Discard route`).
- **The depth gate's standoff is measured, never sampled** (2026-10-20): every guard reads the traced 3 m line through one `GateRule`, a reading is a lower bound on the true distance, and the gate waives itself within 20 m of an end alone — every drawn straight past that, the ends' own first step and the corner pass's included, keeps the wall ([`261017_FEAT_PLN_Route_gate-standoff-sampling.md`](261017_FEAT_PLN_Route_gate-standoff-sampling.md), [`261020_FEAT_PLN_Route_gate-enforcement.md`](261020_FEAT_PLN_Route_gate-enforcement.md)).
- **A zone a route end stands in is no forced crossing** (2026-10-21): the forced-crossing probe leaves that zone's water open and never names it, so the corridor growth fires only on a crossing forced elsewhere — an end walled in by its own zone had made every rung grow a corridor that could not change the answer, ~10 s of a 19–21 s search on the device ([`261021_FEAT_PLN_Route_pull-cost.md`](261021_FEAT_PLN_Route_pull-cost.md)).
- A pair whose ends stand closer than `route.min.acquisition.lengthM` refuses to arm, with a toast, rather than being searched.
- The drawer's eligible ends are built from the unfiltered marker source `allMarkers`, so no list filter narrows what the two selectors offer.
- A saved route opens from the track card's resume slot as the active route; its exit dialog is already in the **written** state — the save door disabled, the third door reading **Leave** rather than **Discard route** (R59, R100).
- A route's time is the plan's time, wherever it is stored or shown: no path derives a time from distance ÷ pace while a plan for that line exists, and a draft that never sees a plan is written with **no time** rather than a paced one.
- A speed limit's figure counts only the time spent on water where that limit is in force — the part of a leg that lies inside the band or a zone — an open-water slow leg charged to **no entry**, and the band its own bucket rather than the nearest limit's.
- The routing's depth read takes the chart's own EMODnet shallow cutoff (`emodnetShallowCutoffM`, shipped default `depth.emodnetShallowCutoffM` = 3.0): an EMODnet reading it excludes, and a node with no data, enter the router's blend **at the gate depth** (`DepthGrid.depthAtFilled`, 2026-10-20), so neither walls a chord nor drags a real sounding beside it under the gate, while a shallow **fine-source** cell still walls. The rule's one home is `DepthSource.isExcludedEmodnet`.
- The acquisition's third door reads its own short `Discard` / `Abandonner` (`route_acq_discard`) while the selected line is unwritten — it holds a third of the footer row — and the exit dialog's own `Leave` / `Quitter` once the line is a track; both presses defer (R92), the first on the discard's own toast and the second on `route_left_toast`.
