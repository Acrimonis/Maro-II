# Feature: Route

State doc — what the mode is, what the code does today, and what is left. Present tense only: the
`## Implemented` log, the closed walk levels and the struck requirements were removed on the user's word
of 2026-10-07, so this file states the current code and nothing else.

## Concept

Set a destination and have the app draw the route from the boat's position to it, then save that line as
an ordinary track.

- **The two ends are the drawer's** — the hamburger menu's Route sub-section under Navigation holds one dropdown per end, persisted per navigation mode; the start offers the current position, the marker position and every marker flagged as an origin, the destination the marker position and every marker flagged as a destination.
- **An end is read when the action is pressed** — where either end is the current position or the marker position, its value is the corresponding point **at the time of the route action**, and nothing re-reads it afterwards.
- **Arming computes at once** — the drawer's action and the map's route fan, whose `Route` and `Route (auto)` children open the same trigger on the pair that stands when they are pressed, are the doors; the fan's parent only opens the arc. Nothing re-asks afterwards, so one arming holds one answer and no line is ever replaced from inside the mode — with one exception, the **loss mirror** (R99): while a route is followed its two ends reverse **in place** when the reading loses ground against its own recent low, the drawn line and its clock untouched and no search re-run.
- **The engine offers three routes at once** — the ladder computes three fixed aversions (around · best · fast) as separate lookups over one shared grid, each row printing its own distance and ETA, and the engine ranks the rungs that have landed to name the winner (R98).
- **The drawing is the selected line, reinforced by shape and opacity** — the selected line is drawn at the route's own transparency during the acquisition and once confirmed, with a derived under-stroke beneath it (R93), and every line beside it wears the one shared dimming key; while followed the line splits at the boat, so the run behind wears that same dimming key and the run ahead stays at full strength under the edge; **at the destination nothing is left ahead** — fewer than two points is zero points — so the covered line alone stands, in that same dimming key. **No ladder of superseded lines exists.**
- **The acquisition owns three outcomes** — `Save to track` (the selected line, greyed once written), `Select route` (navigation on it, the other routes dropped) and the red `Discard route` (leaves through the deferred discard, R92). Once selected there is no panel at all: the ordinary dashboard returns, and the mode's presence is the line, the toggle and the one exit dialog `Save Route to Track · Continue route · Discard Route`.
- **Speeds come from where the line is** — in open water the pace is the free-water pace setting; at a point inside the 300 m band or a regulated speed zone the speed is that limit, the strictest one where both hold, and the clock follows the limit in force. The band's own limit is read **whatever its price switch says**: `route.avoid.zone300.enabled` prices water, it never suspends a limit. The **dummy's fixed 15 kn** is the placeholder's own fiction and the one place this does not hold.
- **An end the boat stands on may need moving** — where the origin or the destination is the boat's own position and that point is not valid water (land, or shallower than the minimum depth), the point is **moved to the nearest valid water on the sea side**.
- **A saved route is an ordinary track carrying the `route` flag** — listed, exported, drawn and deleted like any other.
- **While a route is followed the distance tile becomes the route's own** — its **main figure is the time left**, the bare ETA in force, over one bottom line carrying the distance left, the Driving preference's own word and the cruising pace (`route_trip_line_fmt`). The plan's age is not shown, and neither is a forced-crossing or budget note: this cell reads the trip and nothing else. The two are **one cell the boat can flip**: a tap on the trip tile returns it to the shore reading and a tap there brings the trip back, a freshly armed route opening on the trip face — and with no route the cell is the ordinary distance tile, untappable. The trip face wears a **border pulsing in the route toggle's own blue** (`AppConfig.routeNavigateColor`), beating on the app's single pulse home; the border marks the route rather than the cell, so the shore reading is drawn with none. The trip cell and the drawer band alike read `remainingFrom(boat)` off **whatever orientation the plan stands in** (R99), so the loss mirror's ETA and distance face the new destination with no second arithmetic.

## Current state of the code

- **The seam** — [`RouteEngine.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteEngine.kt:37) declares the computations, the two ids and the declarations a refusal carries; [`RouteUpdate`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteEngine.kt:167) carries the finished-then-next stage pair, the line so far, the settled result, the reason, a stage's own readings, a rung's provisional pair and the ladder's **running best** (R98), and [`RouteRunningBest`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteEngine.kt:150) is that running best's own value.
- **Four engine rows ship** — the `dummy` is one straight segment at its own fixed 15 kn, ready on construction, judging nothing and answering a null progress flow ([`RouteDummyEngine.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteDummyEngine.kt:65)); the `avoid` crosses five boundaries — corridor · grid · search · pull · snap — publishing each one's stage and geometry, the raw cell chain at pull and the pulled line at snap ([`RouteAvoidEngine.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteAvoidEngine.kt)); `evolutive` shares that whole pipeline, differing by its plan alone — 100 m coarse, 20 m fine, and **no second pass** ([`RouteEvolutiveEngine.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteEvolutiveEngine.kt:30)); and `selective` shares it too, its fine layer a union of four thin collars (the shoreline, the band's outer boundary, the zone rims, the shallow wall) with a conservative per-metre depth preference beside the gate ([`RouteSelectiveEngine.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteSelectiveEngine.kt:30)). `route.engine.id` names the shipped one and an unclaimed id falls back to it.
- **The ladder is ranked, and the winner is preselected** (R98) — [`RoutePreference.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePreference.kt:1) is the ladder's one home for its three λ values, indices and words; [`RoutePassRanking.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePassRanking.kt:1) ranks the rungs that have landed — Fast the total time, Best the total time under `route.avoid.speedZone.timeBudgetPct`'s gate, Fun the absolute zone seconds with the clock last, one shared tail on a lead tie and a total tie to the rung nearest the preference; and the engine folds that ranking at every rung's terminal into the **running best** the update carries. The seat follows that winner and freezes on a hand touch; the fan's `Route auto` child takes the final best.
- **The shared layer every engine stands on is `multipass`** — tagged costed cells, an 8-neighbour A\*, a source-parameterized taut pull, a berth carve, one cost field whose base is always set and whose sources only add, the racing-line corner pass, the clock that obeys the limit in force, the tangent corners, the zone geometry and the four pass seats ([`spatial/multipass/`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass)). **The plan seam sits over it** ([`RouteGridPlan.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridPlan.kt:30)): each engine's own walk cell and fine cell are its plan's decision, never the pipeline's. **A two-layer walk reads the fine cell over the coarse copy** — where a passable fine cell stands over the same water as a coarse cell, the fine cell is the cell the search reads and the coarse copy is not read, while land on the fine grid leaves the coarse cell its ordinary role; so `evolutive`'s coastal ribbon and `selective`'s collars are fine-only water and their lines and clocks follow it, and `avoid`'s single-grid walk is untouched.
- **The fine layer is cached as fixed tiles on the anchored lattice** — [`FineTile.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTile.kt) holds a tile's collar members sparsely, [`TileKey.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/TileKey.kt) keys it on every value its rasterisation reads, and [`FineTileMap.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTileMap.kt) builds each once behind a byte ceiling with LRU eviction and an in-flight guard — so a per-arm fine window is assembled by copying the tiles that cover it, the arm's own grid takes the berth carve, and a second arming marks only the tiles a farther route newly needs.
- **The view model is the mode's whole state** — Idle · Choosing · Following with no arrival state, one worker per ask, the plan and its `remainingFrom` projection, the seat and its freeze, the running best, the session's route-to-track link and the save predicate ([`RouteViewModel.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteViewModel.kt:349)).
- **One file touches osmdroid** — the pool and the pin are attached once and mutated in place, and the paint order is applied as a rank over the whole list so nothing is pinned by position ([`RouteHost.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteHost.kt:69), [`OverlayZOrder.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/OverlayZOrder.kt:85)).
- **The panel is the acquisition's whole surface** — the header's status reads the engine's stage and the **selected route's own number** (`Pull #2`), with no figures line beside it, the sentence line carries a refusal or the no-route word, the summary table pages laterally through [`SwipePager`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/components/SwipePager.kt:34) under the header's page dots, and three bottom-anchored actions close it ([`RouteConfirmPanel.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteConfirmPanel.kt:343)). Each rung's row carries a **provisional** distance and ETA from its first taut line, read off the pulled line at the pull → snap boundary and replaced by the settled figures when that rung lands (R96); the running best's row wears a *so far* mark under its name while the ladder computes, **no marker beyond the selection itself**; and every row prints its time first and its distance second (R98).

- **Every value lives in the properties file** — the line's colour, transparency and width, the shared dimming key, the navigate colour, the pin and the avoid family's margins, gates and prices, all read through [`AppConfig`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/config/AppConfig.kt:126); the app's own rows are the engine dropdown, the free-water pace, the Driving preference, the slow-water budget, the **Active route** colour and the route rendering rows.
- **Still in the code and on the removal list** — the refused-end crosshair with its `route.target.*` keys (replaced by the sea-side move above), the route opacity-ladder keys `tracking.transparency.routeFrom` / `routeTo` (the dimming key covers the need), the panel's own pin checkbox [`RoutePinOption`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt:504) with its `route_pin_label` key (composed by nothing), and the seam members the current flow does not lean on, which the user will re-evaluate.

### Placement and the three seams

The route sits in the app's own slicing rather than in a package of its own, and the isolation survives as three seams on shared files:

1. `MapScreen.kt` — the engine resolved at one expression, one `RouteHost(...)` call, the panel composed into the dashboard slot, the exit doors.
2. The drawer — the Route sub-section and the read-only mode summary, both inside the Navigation card.
3. The Settings overlay — the engine's row, the free-water pace, the Driving preference, the slow-water budget and the **Active route** colour, held by `SettingsManager`.

### The mode's flow

```text
Idle → a door armed on the standing pair → Choosing (the search runs; the panel and the provisional line)
Choosing → Select route → Following (the line, the toggle, the one exit dialog)
Choosing → Save to track / Discard route → Idle
Following → the toggle's off or the back key → the one dialog → save and exit · continue · discard
```

A discard is **two-phase** (R92): the display ends at once and the disposal waits on the undo toast.

Arrival is a cue and no state (R100): when the time-to-go falls through the arrival threshold the exit
dialog is raised under a *reached your destination* prompt, and the trip cell still reads zero while the
line stays drawn.

### Decoupling rules

1. Route depends only on `data/model`'s `LatLng`, `RoutePoint` and `RouteResult`, the shared `spatial/Units.kt` and `SpatialOperations`, the `RouteEngine` seam, and the shared `ui.color.reinforcedColor` helper (R93).
2. An engine that wants the water reads it through **its own** world interface; the dummy reads none at all.
3. The feature declares no conversion constant, no baked artifact and no dependency of its own, and the zone and band values stay the RegulatedZones layer's.
4. All Route runtime state lives in `RouteViewModel` (`StateFlow`), and `RouteHost` is the only file touching osmdroid.

## Requirements

The live numbered requirements — added after the master book was retired; the archived book's own R1–R81 are its history. The code cites these numbers by ordinal, so their home is here.

- **R82 — A saved route's two ends persist on the track and its summary** — `Track.routeStartMarkerId` and `Track.routeDestinationMarkerId` at proto 20/21, an **empty string** meaning *this end was not a marker*, and the index pass projects both into `TrackSummary` beside `route` so a summary reads them **without loading a track**. Only a flagged-marker end carries an identity.
- **R88 — A route is selectable while its line is still drawing** — `Select route` opens once the main lookup has a drawable partial line (≥ 2 provisional points), and the press **commits** the main instead of freezing it: the candidate lookups are cancelled, the main keeps running, and the mode enters `Following` with the landed line the moment it lands; a refusal un-commits rather than following.
- **R89 — A route is saveable while its line is still drawing** — `Save to track` opens on the same partial line, writes it immediately, and **re-saves the same track id at each main iteration** — a fixed id, `createdAtMs` and name, only the points and legs growing — until the landed full line is written once more and the session links the landed plan to the draft, shutting the save door.
- **R90 — The acquisition panel is the shared dashboard scaffold with a paging summary table** — the panel rides [`DrawerScaffold`](app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/components/DrawerScaffold.kt:149) with `showBack = false`, `wrapContent = !isLandscape` and `wrapContentMinHeight = portraitDashboardHeight`, so it auto-grows to its content instead of scrolling internally; the header carries the title and, at its trailing edge, the stage status — the stage's own word and the selected route's number, `<stage> #<n>` — and the page dots; the body is a bordered three-column table — the rung names (0.75 of the comparison column), the route's ETA · Dist as value · unit pairs (value right-aligned, unit left-aligned, a placeholder while it waits), and a candidate's delta against the selected route with its Speed limits grid — with a hairline between the columns, wrapping top-aligned rows, the selected row on the taken-choice face (a `ui.select.container` fill, its `ui.accent` edge, white bold text) whose corners adapt to the row's position, and the whole table paging laterally by swipe or a row tap; the three actions — Save · Select · Discard — share one weighted bottom row, and the destination-moved note follows only while a plan stands.
- **R91 — The route fan closes on a child press only for acquire and follow** — pressing a child fires that child's action; the **acquire** (`Route`) and **follow** (`Route auto`) children also close the arc, while **Discard**, **Save+Exit** and **Save** leave it expanded, and otherwise the fan closes on the back key, a tap on the scrim, or the parent anchor's own toggle, the sibling fan's faded anchor being a known limit aside.
- **R92 — Every route discard is two-phase: the display ends at once, the disposal waits on the undo toast** — a discarding press — the panel's `Discard route`, the fan's Discard, the toggle-off and the back key inside the acquisition, and the exit dialog's own Discard while Following — sets a `pendingDiscard` window instead of disposing: the acquisition panel and the route line leave and the toggle reads off, while `routeArmed`/`routeState` stay live and the engine keeps running underneath. The real disposal — `endRouteMode()` plus the engine's `cancelLookup` — runs when the toast is dismissed: its timeout, a horizontal swipe, the second back press, or **New acquisition**; **Undo** clears the window and the panel, line, toggle and selection return with nothing recomputed. **A new arming commits then arms**, the fan's Save/Select act on the live mode and supersede the window, and the route toast jumps the snackbar queue. Every explicit arming passes the force-fresh flag, so the acquisition always searches the standing ends afresh.
- **R93 — The selected route is reinforced by shape and opacity** — a **derived edge** is drawn beneath the selected line in both phases — the selected rung during the acquisition and the followed line while `Following` — its colour the line's own pushed toward black by the one lever `ui.reinforce.darkenPct` (read through `AppConfig.uiReinforceDarkenPct`, clamped 0..100) and its width the key `route.line.casing.widthDp`, reusing the core width's own `1f / 3f..24f` bounds; the edge takes the line's own transparency, so **no colour, brightness or alpha key** is added. `reinforcedColor` is pure and RGB-only with the **caller** reading the lever. While `Following`, slot 0 draws the split's remaining run alone — never the whole line beneath the travelled overlay — the casing mirrors exactly what slot 0 draws, and `route_travelled` carries the covered run whenever it is a real run; **where fewer than two points remain the run is empty** — zero points is nothing ahead — so slot 0 and its casing stand down and the travelled run alone draws the covered line in the shared dimming, the paint's one face rule. The word **arrival** is the cue's alone (R100): this paint case is an empty remaining run, never a state. `RoutePlan.splitAt(from)` splits the line at the boat — the travelled run wearing `route.dimmed.transparencyPct` and the remaining run at full strength under the edge — and `remainingFrom` reads the same projection, so it has one home. **The read direction is not an identity** (R99): the split, the travelled run, the casing and the destination pin are all read off the plan object, so a mirrored plan repaints them in the same stroke and nothing re-searches.
- **R94 — The seat never parks on an empty row** — [`routeSeatedIndex(hasPlan, preferredIndex)`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt:247) answers the preferred page when it holds a plan, otherwise the page **holding a plan** nearest the preferred one (the lower index winning an even split), otherwise the preferred index unchanged, so no state survives in which the selection holds nothing while a line stands beside it. Arming runs the same predicate from the Driving preference's rung. The seat is `RouteViewModel.selectedIndex`, while `RouteState.Choosing.plan` is the **main's** plan (index 0).
- **R95 — The ladder reads as a hurry↔fun axis: Fast · Best · Fun, ordered by ETA** — the three stops are labelled `Fast`/`Rapide`, `Best`/`Optimal` and `Fun`/`Plaisir` in both locales; the acquisition presents the rungs in landed-ETA ascending order, ties keeping the ladder's natural order, while the provisional line and the stage stay on the main; and the seat resolves the preferred rung from the **live** `routeSlowWaterAversion`.
- **R96 — A rung's row carries a provisional distance and ETA from its first taut line, replaced by the settled figures when the rung lands** — the pair is taken **at the pull → snap boundary**, from the **pulled** line alone, by one enforced-limit read of that line ([`timeLineWithLimits`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteEta.kt:73)) plus its own length — **never** from the raw cell chain, whose staircase overestimates. It rides the boundary update that already carries the line ([`RouteUpdate.provisional`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/RouteEngine.kt:191)): the narrating main emits it on its SNAP update, and a **candidate** emits it on a boundary update of its own, the stage word staying the main's alone. The row holds it per page — `RoutePage.provisional`, keyed by the lookup/computation id — and **clears it when that rung's terminal update lands**. **The provisional clock is the enforced-limit clock; the settled time is the profile clock.**
- **R97 — The device pass is evidence, not a build order** — a pass has two jobs. As an **acceptance** it shows an optimisation changed nothing the user sees — the same line, distance and clock — where the unit suite only proves arithmetic on fixtures. As a **measurement** it prints the figure that prices the saving. It is the **user's own** and the agent never touches the device. It is **not a precondition for a code landing** — every change is built and judged on fixtures and the unit suite — and only the steps whose subject *is* a reading wait on it.
- **R98 — The preference states an intent, the engine ranks its rungs, and the seat follows until a hand touch freezes it** — the Driving preference states **how the three criteria are weighed**: **Fast** the trip's total time, **Best** the total time provided the zone share stays within `route.avoid.speedZone.timeBudgetPct`, **Fun** the absolute zone seconds with the clock only as the last resort. The engine folds the rungs that have landed through [`RoutePassRanking`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePassRanking.kt:1) at **every rung's terminal**, riding the terminal update as `RouteRunningBest` (the winner's lookup id and how many it compared), so a surface reads *so far* rather than a verdict; a rung that found no path is no candidate. The seat **follows the running best on every improvement** and **a touch of the selection freezes it for good**. The fan's `Route auto` child waits for the settled set and takes the **final best**. The winner's row wears a *so far* mark under its name while the ladder still computes and **no disc** — the selection alone marks the chosen line, the disc having been dropped on the user's word as distracting — and the row's two figures print **time first, distance second** for a landed row and a waiting one alike. Best's gate has a Routing-settings row over the stored budget.
- **R99 — A followed route loses ground and mirrors its two ends in place** — while `Following`, the reading **toggles** once the trip figure's own time-to-go (`remainingFrom(boat).durationSec`) is `route.follow.swap.lossSec` worse than its own recent low; a fall or a flat reading is the ordinary progressing state and does nothing, and the toggle is **never gated by the arrival threshold** — what keeps it quiet at the destination is the number's own flatness, clamped to the route's end. The low is the smallest sample inside a look-back of **twice the loss** (derived, not a dial), so the route turns the moment the loss accrues while a drift slower than half the route's own pace never counts; the history is bucketed to about one a second on `SystemClock` elapsed-realtime, **cleared at every flip** — the jump to the other end's value belongs to the other orientation — and on entering `Following`, with `route.follow.swap.debounceSec` spacing the toggles. The predicate is one pure function, [`routeEtaToggle`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt), with [`routeEtaStep`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt) advancing the history, and the mirror one plan method, [`RoutePlan.reversed`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteViewModel.kt). It is **in place only**: `points` and `legTimesSec` reverse and the two ends exchange while every settled total rides unchanged, so the drawn geometry and the clock stay byte-identical and no lookup runs — [`RouteViewModel.onBoatFix`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteViewModel.kt) is fed the map's own fix and nothing re-arms. The route keeps its **identity**: the session's route-to-track link, `isRouteSaved` and the persisted end ids (R82) do not fork with the read direction, so a mirrored saved route lists and reopens as the same route.
- **R100 — The arrival cue rides the exit dialog's own doors** — when `remainingFrom(boat).durationSec` falls through `route.follow.arrival.etaSec` while still closing, **as a new look-back low**, the mode raises the exit dialog ([`MapDialogHost`](app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/MapDialogHost.kt)) under a title-only *reached your destination* prompt (`route_arrival_title`, no message), with the same doors — *Save Route to Track · Continue route · Discard Route*, or *Stop following* for a followed saved route. The cue is **armed on entering `Following` from above the threshold, and armed again by every flip** — so a route taken up already inside the zone raises no prompt at the outset, while the mirrored end behaves exactly like the first and both ends of the route ask — and it **disarms on firing and re-arms whenever the time-to-go reads back above the threshold**, so a boat that leaves and comes back down, or turns for the other end, legitimately re-prompts: the same loss-versus-low test decides the flip and the prompt alike, and no second dial is invented. The doors that keep the route — *Continue route* and a dismissal (no action) — leave the mode `Following`, while the doors that end it make the flip moot. **A reversal outranks the prompt**: if the loss accrues while the cue is open, the cue is closed as a **no action** dismissal and the flip proceeds. One surface, two raisers: a raise while the dialog stands is that one dialog with the user's own title, and the arrival raiser never stacks a second.

## Rules

- A pair whose ends stand closer than `route.min.acquisition.lengthM` refuses to arm, with a toast, rather than being searched.
- The drawer's eligible ends are built from the unfiltered marker source `allMarkers`, so no list filter narrows what the two selectors offer.
- A saved route opens from the track card's resume slot as the active route, and that session's exit dialog reads **Stop following**.
