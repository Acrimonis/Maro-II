# Feature: Route

State doc — what the mode is, what the code does today, and what is left. Present tense only: the
`## Implemented` log, the closed walk levels and the struck requirements were removed on the user's word
of 2026-10-07, so this file states the current code and nothing else.

## Concept

Set a destination and have the app draw the route from the boat's position to it, then save that line as
an ordinary track.

- **The two ends are the drawer's** — the hamburger menu's Route sub-section under Navigation holds one dropdown per end, persisted per navigation mode; the start offers the current position, the marker position and every marker flagged as an origin, the destination the marker position and every marker flagged as a destination.
- **An end is read when the action is pressed** — where either end is the current position or the marker position, its value is the corresponding point **at the time of the route action**, and nothing re-reads it afterwards.
- **Arming computes at once** — the drawer's action and the map's route fan, whose `Route` and `Route (auto)` children open the same trigger on the pair that stands when they are pressed, are the doors; the fan's `Follow` child takes the settled or partial line once it stands, and the fan's parent only opens the arc. Nothing re-asks afterwards, so one arming holds one answer and no line is ever replaced from inside the mode — with one exception, the **loss mirror** (R99): while a route is followed its two ends reverse **in place** when the reading loses ground against its own recent low, the drawn line and its clock untouched and no search re-run.
- **The engine offers three routes at once** — the ladder computes three fixed aversions (around · best · fast) as separate lookups over one shared grid, each row printing its own distance and ETA, and the engine crowns the lowest score among the rungs that have landed (R98). **Each rung's λ is a key of its own** — `route.avoid.ladder.around|best|fast.lambda`, read through `AppConfig` and published by `RoutePreference`, which now holds the three rungs' λ alone — and the **crown is one sum over the three drawn lines**: the `Slow water` dial states how many minutes a minute in slow water counts as (**Fastest** · 1x · 2x · 3x · 4x · **Ignore time**), and the ranking crowns the lowest trip time plus that weight times the line's own seconds under a limit.
- **The drawing is the selected line, reinforced by shape and opacity** — the selected line is drawn at the route's own transparency during the acquisition and once confirmed, with a derived under-stroke beneath it (R93), and every line beside it wears the one shared dimming key; while followed the line splits at the boat, so the run behind wears that same dimming key and the run ahead stays at full strength under the edge; **at the destination nothing is left ahead** — fewer than two points is zero points — so the covered line alone stands, in that same dimming key. **No ladder of superseded lines exists.**
- **The acquisition owns three outcomes** — `Save to track` (the selected line, greyed once written), `Select route` (navigation on it, the other routes dropped) and the red `Discard route` (leaves through the deferred discard, R92). Once selected there is no panel at all: the ordinary dashboard returns, and the mode's presence is the line, the toggle and the one exit dialog — `Save and Leave` · `Continue` · `Discard route` while the line is unwritten, the save door disabled and the third reading `Leave` once it is a track (R59, R100). That third door is **two-state like the dialog's own**: while the selected line is unwritten it reads the acquisition's own short `Discard` / `Abandonner` (`route_acq_discard`) — it holds a third of the weighted footer row, where the dialog's longer loss word would not fit — and once the line is a track it hands over to the exit dialog's own `Leave` / `Quitter`, accent and not red, derived from `routeExitDoors(written = true).loss`, its word following the cost, never the surface. The word moves, the act does not: every ending of the panel and of the dialog leaves through the **deferred window** (R92), raising `route_left_toast` — *Route left* / *Route quittée*.
- **Speeds come from where the line is** — in open water the pace is the free-water pace setting — its first stop, `Any`, planning at the boat's own pace and falling back to the shipped value until the boat has evidence; at a point inside the 300 m band or a regulated speed zone the speed is that limit, the strictest one where both hold, and the clock follows the limit in force. The band's own limit is read **whatever its price switch says**: `route.avoid.zone300.enabled` prices water, it never suspends a limit. The **dummy's fixed 15 kn** is the placeholder's own fiction and the one place this does not hold.
- **An end the boat stands on may need moving** — where the origin or the destination is the boat's own position and that point is not valid water (land, or shallower than the minimum depth), the point is **moved to the nearest valid water on the sea side**.
- **A saved route is an ordinary track carrying the `route` flag** — listed, exported, drawn and deleted like any other.
- **While a route is followed the distance tile becomes the route's own** — its **main figure is the time left**, the bare ETA in force, over one bottom line carrying the distance left, the `Slow water` dial's own word and the cruising pace (`route_trip_line_fmt`). The plan's age is not shown, and neither is a forced-crossing note: this cell reads the trip and nothing else. The two are **one cell the boat can flip**: a tap on the trip tile returns it to the shore reading and a tap there brings the trip back, a freshly armed route opening on the trip face — and with no route the cell is the ordinary distance tile, untappable. The cell wears a **border pulsing in the route toggle's own blue** (`AppConfig.routeNavigateColor`), beating on the app's single pulse home; the border marks the followed route rather than one face, so the trip reading and the shore reading alike wear it and one beat spans the flip. The trip cell and the drawer band alike read `remainingFrom(boat)` off **whatever orientation the plan stands in** (R99), so the loss mirror's ETA and distance face the new destination with no second arithmetic.

The seat follows that winner and freezes on a hand touch; the fan's `Route auto` child takes the final best.
- **The shared layer every engine stands on is `multipass`** — tagged costed cells, an 8-neighbour A\*, a source-parameterized taut pull, a berth carve, one cost field whose base is always set and whose sources only add, the racing-line corner pass, the clock that obeys the limit in force, the tangent corners, the zone geometry and the four pass seats ([`spatial/multipass/`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass)). **The plan seam sits over it** ([`RouteGridPlan.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridPlan.kt:30)): each engine's own walk cell and fine cell are its plan's decision, never the pipeline's, and the seam also says **which depth band its water pays** — [`RouteGridPlan.depthBand()`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridPlan.kt:72) answers `null` for `avoid` and the two `route.evolutive.depth.*` keys for `evolutive`, the field computing the seconds and the plan naming them (2026-10-16). **A two-layer walk reads the fine cell over the coarse copy** — where a passable fine cell stands over the same water as a coarse cell, the fine cell is the cell the search reads and the coarse copy is not read, while land on the fine grid leaves the coarse cell its ordinary role; so `evolutive`'s coastal ribbon is fine-only water and its line and clock follow it, and `avoid`'s single-grid walk is untouched.
- **The fine layer is cached as pace-free tiles on the anchored lattice, behind an on-disk store** — [`FineTile.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTile.kt) holds a tile's collar members sparsely, its one price-bearing double a **pace-free per-member factor** (the base is re-applied once, from the target grid's own base, when the tile is materialised), so one tile serves every speed; [`TileKey.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/TileKey.kt) keys it on every value its rasterisation reads **but the pace**, [`FineTileCodec.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTileCodec.kt) writes a **versioned** entry (v2 carries the factor; a v1 entry is a miss, never migrated), [`DirectoryFineTileStore.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/DirectoryFineTileStore.kt) persists them on disk behind the `route.fineTile.maxBytes` ceiling — 96 MiB, and a **soft** one: the warm-up's home area rides a `protect` set, so the store reclaims its **least recently used unprotected** tiles from one **access-ordered index** — no save lists or sorts the directory — takes no prepared home tile, and reports once when only protected bytes are left above it, while `list()` keeps reading the directory because the warm-up treats a name as proof a file stands — the app edge raising that first reclamation as a toast **and** pushing it as a notification on the app's single channel, behind one `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` ask at startup — and [`FineTileMap.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTileMap.kt) builds each once behind a byte ceiling with LRU eviction and an in-flight guard, counts a store-served lookup a hit and writes each build back — so a per-arm fine window is assembled by copying the tiles that cover it, the arm's own grid takes the berth carve, and a second arming marks only the tiles a farther route newly needs.
- **A startup warm-up fills those tiles before an arm asks for them, then grows outward until the phone or the map says stop** — [`FineTilePrewarm.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTilePrewarm.kt) walks the plan's own tiles through the **builder's own key and build** ([`RouteGridBuilder.fineTilesOver`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteGridBuilder.kt:458), the arm's `fineTileKey` and `buildFineTile`), so a tile it makes is the very tile an arm over that water asks for — never a second derivation. It is **store-first** — a warm tile is skipped by name before any build, so a fully warm ring builds nothing and opens no window at all — and ordered **own-ship outward** from the last known position (the seed area's centre as fallback). [`FineTileWarm.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/FineTileWarm.kt) seeds it on that area (`route.prewarm.box`, the Cagnes-sur-Mer → Cannes coast) and takes that seed ring **handed in** by the app edge, which derives it once and protects its entry names before the walk's first fullness check — so protection never depends on a stop — and grows the area **outward in rings** while the store has room, stopping on the store's own **room under the target share of its ceiling** ([`TileStore.fullness()`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/TileStore.kt) read through `StoreFullness.hasRoomAt`, so a live verdict that cannot latch and the rest of the ceiling stands as a reserve for water the seed area does not cover), on the pass's own pack cap, or on the map's baked bounds — a box that falls outside the region is reported rather than shrunk, **no radius dial exists**, and the walk's end line carries the map's hit rate.
- **Its workplace is its own** — the pass runs at the app edge ([`MapRoutePrewarmEffect`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/MapRouteEffects.kt)) on a **worker of its own at a background thread priority**, off the screen's thread, and it starts once both baked layers can answer rather than on the boat being idle: a tile built before the depth grid loads would key `depthGateEnabled = false` and no arm could ever reach it. It **yields** to an armed route and to a depth-raster regeneration, both re-asked per tile and per ring, and it **stands down** on battery-saver, re-read at every ask; one process-level slot holds one walk, so an Activity recreation's successor waits rather than starting a second.
- **Its white dot is the warm-up's own `busy` window**, opened for each tile the walk really builds and merged across them by the holder's grace (`FineTileCache.beginWarmTile` / `endWarmTile`), so the routing square's mark stands for the walk's work rather than flickering across its tiles, goes **dark the moment the walk waits at its gate** — an armed route, a competing heavy job or battery-saver — and lights nothing at all where every ring is already warm. The map reports no per-lookup activity, so an arm's own store reads never light it.
- **The store answers how full it is, live, and under which share** — [`TileStore.fullness()`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/TileStore.kt) hands the bytes held, the ceiling and **the size of the entry it wrote last**, and [`StoreFullness.hasRoomAt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/TileStore.kt) answers whether one more entry of that size still fits **under a share of the ceiling** — the `route.prewarm.targetPct` the warm-up holds its growth to, so the rest of the ceiling stands as a reserve for water the seed area does not cover; `StoreFullness.atCeiling` is that question asked in full. - **A costed marker prices the sea as a well and a Blocked one walls it** — the acquisition reads the user markers through the world's own projection ([`RouteMarker.kt`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteMarker.kt:1)): a **Circle**, a **Corridor** or a **Pin** carrying a cost 1–9 adds a **λ-free price** — `base × step × strength` times the geometry's own **well factor**, full inside the marker's body and falling in a straight line to nothing at the outer edge of the marker's own **proximity** — and a `ROUTING_COST_BLOCKED` (10) marker is a **hard wall** the line never crosses over its **body** alone. A **Pin** is a body of radius `0`, so its whole price is a cone from its centre, and it is **never a wall**: its cost tops out at the top price whatever is stored. An auto marker carries no route role or cost at all. Both ride the one cost field, so the A\* and the pull's guard price identically, and the fine layer's tile key carries the costed set **beside the price's own strength and switch**, so a marker move, a strength move or a switch toggle re-tiles. Gated by `route.marker.enabled` (the price alone — a wall stands whatever it says), and the price's own report **names its arm** — `PriceArm.MARKER`, beside the band, the rings and the depth ramp — while the wall declares none, being law rather than a price; a point under several markers sums them into that one arm, so the report names **a** marker and never which one.
- **Still in the code and on the removal list** — the refused-end crosshair with its `route.target.*` keys (replaced by the sea-side move above), the route opacity-ladder keys `tracking.transparency.routeFrom` / `routeTo` (the dimming key covers the need), the panel's own pin checkbox [`RoutePinOption`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt:504) with its `route_pin_label` key (composed by nothing), and the seam members the current flow does not lean on, which the user will re-evaluate.
- **Known limits** — the dummy promises nothing about water and no setting moves it; each plan decides its own fine cell in metres: `route.avoid.grid.fineCellM` and `route.evolutive.grid.fineCellM`; and the progressive-draw plan's build order still names `route.progress.transparencyPct`, retired on 2026-09-28 for the shared `path.line.fade.dimmed`, so the plan's text is the stale one and not the tree.
- **One deliberate limit stands** — the Speed-limits line prints **whole minutes** only, dropping a sub-minute entry so it never prints a zero-minute row. The depth price's own **limit**, its ramp quantized to the fine cell, left with the engine that carried it; the depth band that returned on 2026-10-16 is a **price** and adds no limit, so the clock, the attribution and the table are unmoved by it.

- **The corner pass rounds a line's bends from what the line gives it** — the fit is a clothoid → arc → clothoid whose reach takes the whole **significant** leg, a kink too flat to be a corner keeping its own point and taking no room, and the corner floor is a **target rather than a refusal**: the room may draw a bend under it, down to the pass's own 1 kn bottom. A close pair of bends turning the **same way** within the tangent a pace-speed curve needs for their summed angle is read as **one turn**, over the crossing of its two outer legs; two bends on one leg **share** it, each weighted by its own angle; and the overlap guard measures bow to bow, a bow taking back the points it has passed so the drawn line never folds back. The coast test is **relative to the corner it replaces** — a bow may not come closer to the shore than the corner itself, the shallow wall staying absolute — and every bend's answer, its room, its share, the limit in force at it and whether it stands in the band are named by the `CORNERS` and `CORNER` lines the pass prints on the `MaroRoute` trace. Design, measurements and the device evidence live in [`261013_FEAT_PLN_Route_corner-pass-fixes.md`](261013_FEAT_PLN_Route_corner-pass-fixes.md); [`RouteCornerPassTest`](../../app/src/test/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RouteCornerPassTest.kt) pins each rule.

### The mode's flow

```text
Idle → a door armed on the standing pair → Choosing (the search runs; the panel and the provisional line)
Choosing → Select route → Following (the line, the toggle, the one exit dialog)
Choosing → Save to track / Discard route → Idle
Following → the toggle's off or the back key → the one dialog → save and exit · continue · discard
```

A discard is **two-phase** (R92): the display ends at once and the disposal waits on the undo toast.

Arrival is a cue and no state (R100): when the time-to-go falls through the arrival threshold the exit
dialog is raised under a *reached your destination* prompt, and the trip cell still reads zero while the
line stays drawn.

### Decoupling rules

1. Route depends only on `data/model`'s `LatLng`, `RoutePoint` and `RouteResult`, the shared `spatial/Units.kt` and `SpatialOperations`, the `RouteEngine` seam, and the shared `ui.color.reinforcedColor` helper (R93).
2. An engine that wants the water reads it through **its own** world interface; the dummy reads none at all.
3. The feature declares no conversion constant, no baked artifact and no dependency of its own, and the zone and band values stay the RegulatedZones layer's.
4. All Route runtime state lives in `RouteViewModel` (`StateFlow`), and `RouteHost` is the only file touching osmdroid.

## Requirements

The live numbered requirements — added after the master book was retired; the archived book's own R1–R81 are its history. The code cites these numbers by ordinal, so their home is here.

- **R82 — A saved route's two ends persist on the track and its summary** — `Track.routeStartMarkerId` and `Track.routeDestinationMarkerId` at proto 20/21, an **empty string** meaning *this end was not a marker*, and the index pass projects both into `TrackSummary` beside `route` so a summary reads them **without loading a track**. Only a flagged-marker end carries an identity.
- **R88 — A route is selectable while its line is still drawing** — `Select route` opens once the main lookup has a drawable partial line (≥ 2 provisional points), and the press **commits** the main instead of freezing it: the candidate lookups are cancelled, the main keeps running, and the mode enters `Following` with the landed line the moment it lands; a refusal un-commits rather than following.
- **R89 — A route is saveable while its line is still drawing** — `Save to track` opens on the same partial line, writes it immediately, and **re-saves the same track id at each main iteration** — a fixed id, `createdAtMs` and name, only the points and legs growing — until the landed full line is written once more and the session links the landed plan to the draft, shutting the save door.
- **R90 — The acquisition panel is the shared dashboard scaffold with a paging summary table** — the panel rides [`DrawerScaffold`](app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/components/DrawerScaffold.kt:149) with `showBack = false`, `wrapContent = !isLandscape` and `wrapContentMinHeight = portraitDashboardHeight`, so it auto-grows to its content instead of scrolling internally; the header carries the title and, at its trailing edge, the stage status — the stage's own word and the selected route's number, `<stage> #<n>` — and the page dots; the body is a bordered three-column table — the rung names (0.75 of the comparison column), the route's ETA · Dist as value · unit pairs (value right-aligned, unit left-aligned, a placeholder while it waits), and a candidate's delta against the selected route with its Speed limits grid — with a hairline between the columns, wrapping top-aligned rows, the selected row on the taken-choice face (a `ui.select.container` fill, its `ui.accent` edge, white bold text) whose corners adapt to the row's position, and the whole table paging laterally by swipe or a row tap; the three actions — Save · Select · the ending — share one weighted bottom row, the ending door **two-state**: its own short `Discard` / `Abandonner` (`route_acq_discard`) while the line is unwritten and the exit dialog's own `Leave` / `Quitter`, accent and not red, once it is a track; and the destination-moved note follows only while a plan stands.
- **R91 — The route fan closes on a child press only for follow and acquire** — pressing a child fires that child's action; the **follow**, **acquire** (`Route`) and **auto** (`Route auto`) children also close the arc, while **Discard** and **Save** leave it expanded, and otherwise the fan closes on the back key, a tap on the scrim, or the parent anchor's own toggle, the sibling fan's faded anchor being a known limit aside.

- **R97 — The device pass is evidence, not a build order** — a pass has two jobs. As an **acceptance** it shows an optimisation changed nothing the user sees — the same line, distance and clock — where the unit suite only proves arithmetic on fixtures. As a **measurement** it prints the figure that prices the saving. It is the **user's own** and the agent never touches the device. It is **not a precondition for a code landing** — every change is built and judged on fixtures and the unit suite — and only the steps whose subject *is* a reading wait on it.
- **R98 — One dial weighs the crown, a slow second is weighted by its own limit, and the panel shows the order** — the `Slow water` row (`route.avoid.slowWater.weight`, seeded 4, one persisted stop index) states how many minutes a minute in slow water counts as: **Fastest** (weight 0) takes the quickest trip, 1x–4x add that weight times the line's **weighted** seconds under a limit, and **Ignore time** weighs those seconds alone with the clock as its tie. Each second's weight is **linear in its own limit's shortfall** — `(pace − limit) / pace`, floored at zero, so 0.8 at 5 kn and 0.2 at 20 kn on a 25 kn pace and **nothing at or above the pace** — defined once in `slowWaterSeverity()`; the seconds measured are a ring's interior, the 300 m band and the ramps that serve a limit, a bend's own floor outside them, and a leg meeting **no** limit weighs **nothing**. The published per-limit table stays **plain seconds**, so the panel's minutes, the drawing, the clock and the dial are unmoved, while the crown's figure becomes the line's own **excess under its limits** — the quantity the drawing bills. The engine folds the rungs that have landed through [`RoutePassRanking`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/spatial/multipass/RoutePassRanking.kt:1) at **every rung's terminal**, riding the terminal update as `RouteRunningBest` while it computes and as `RouteVerdict` once the set has settled — the crown and every landed rung's **rank**, distinct `1..n`: `route.avoid.ladder.tieToleranceSec` (30 s) decides what counts as a tie and the crown stays that group's head, while the order inside a group runs the **exact score**, then **fewer weighted seconds**, then the **shorter trip**, then the row that **landed first**. The seat **follows the running best on every improvement** and **a touch of the selection freezes it for good**; arming seats the main's page, and the fan's `Route auto` child takes the **final best** once the set has settled. The panel closes the **crowned** row's name with a checkmark in the route accent and puts that row's **rank** on the row's second line — the ` so far ` word's own slot while the ladder computes, one slot and one clock — while the selection keeps the taken-choice face; every row prints **time first, distance second**.
- **R99 — A followed route loses ground and mirrors its two ends in place** — while `Following`, the reading **toggles** once the trip figure's own time-to-go (`remainingFrom(boat).durationSec`) is `route.follow.swap.lossSec` worse than its own recent low; a fall or a flat reading is the ordinary progressing state and does nothing, and the toggle is **never gated by the arrival threshold** — what keeps it quiet at the destination is the number's own flatness, clamped to the route's end. The low is the smallest sample inside a look-back of **twice the loss** (derived, not a dial), so the route turns the moment the loss accrues while a drift slower than half the route's own pace never counts; the history is bucketed to about one a second on `SystemClock` elapsed-realtime, **cleared at every flip** — the jump to the other end's value belongs to the other orientation — and on entering `Following`, with `route.follow.swap.debounceSec` spacing the toggles. The predicate is one pure function, [`routeEtaToggle`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt), with [`routeEtaStep`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteOverlay.kt) advancing the history, and the mirror one plan method, [`RoutePlan.reversed`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteViewModel.kt). It is **in place only**: `points` and `legTimesSec` reverse and the two ends exchange while every settled total rides unchanged, so the drawn geometry and the clock stay byte-identical and no lookup runs — [`RouteViewModel.onBoatFix`](../../app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/RouteViewModel.kt) is fed the map's own fix and nothing re-arms. The route keeps its **identity**: the session's route-to-track link, `isRouteSaved` and the persisted end ids (R82) do not fork with the read direction, so a mirrored saved route lists and reopens as the same route.
- **R100 — The arrival cue rides the exit dialog's own doors** — when `remainingFrom(boat).durationSec` falls through `route.follow.arrival.etaSec` while still closing, **as a new look-back low**, the mode raises the exit dialog ([`MapDialogHost`](app/src/main/java/ykws/android/maro/ui/map/MapDialogHost.kt)) under a title-only *reached your destination* prompt (`route_arrival_title`, no message), with the same three doors at every state, decided by one axis — **is this line already a track?**: while the line is **unwritten** *Save route and exit* (accent, enabled) · *Continue* (secondary) · *Discard route* (red); once it is **written** the save door keeps its place and is **disabled** · *Continue* (secondary) · *Leave* (the accent, and **not red**, because nothing is lost) — the third door's word following the **cost** rather than the raiser. The cue is **armed on entering `Following` from above the threshold, and armed again by every flip** — so a route taken up already inside the zone raises no prompt at the outset, while the mirrored end behaves exactly like the first and both ends of the route ask — and it **disarms on firing and re-arms whenever the time-to-go reads back above the threshold**, so a boat that leaves and comes back down, or turns for the other end, legitimately re-prompts: the same loss-versus-low test decides the flip and the prompt alike, and no second dial is invented. The doors that keep the route — *Continue* and a dismissal (no action) — leave the mode `Following`, while the doors that end it make the flip moot. **A reversal outranks the prompt**: if the loss accrues while the cue is open, the cue is closed as a **no action** dismissal and the flip proceeds. One surface, two raisers: a raise while the dialog stands is that one dialog with the user's own title, and the arrival raiser never stacks a second.

## Rules

- A pair whose ends stand closer than `route.min.acquisition.lengthM` refuses to arm, with a toast, rather than being searched.
- The drawer's eligible ends are built from the unfiltered marker source `allMarkers`, so no list filter narrows what the two selectors offer.
- A saved route opens from the track card's resume slot as the active route; its exit dialog is already in the **written** state — the save door disabled, the third door reading **Leave** rather than **Discard route** (R59, R100).
- A route's time is the plan's time, wherever it is stored or shown: no path derives a time from distance ÷ pace while a plan for that line exists, and a draft that never sees a plan is written with **no time** rather than a paced one.
- A speed limit's figure counts only the time spent on water where that limit is in force — the part of a leg that lies inside the band or a zone — an open-water slow leg charged to **no entry**, and the band its own bucket rather than the nearest limit's.
- The routing's depth read passes the chart's own EMODnet shallow cutoff (`emodnetShallowCutoffM`), so a shallow **chart** cell does not wall a chord while a shallow **fine-source** cell still does.
- The acquisition's third door reads its own short `Discard` / `Abandonner` (`route_acq_discard`) while the selected line is unwritten — it holds a third of the footer row — and the exit dialog's own `Leave` / `Quitter` once the line is a track; both presses defer (R92), the first on the discard's own toast and the second on `route_left_toast`.
